### 1.2 Why the view's L_weak is not honest on M3

Each point names its evidence. Each counterexample is a model trace that the refuter would find against the M2-shaped statement over M3.

**1. Time is not monotone.** `block.timestamp` is part of each step's `HEnv`, and `Reach.RStep` accepts any `HAct`.

The commits set `finalizeTimestamp = t + F + (inChallenge ? (W + start) − t : 0)`. This is facts `commitBatch` write seq 65; `commitState` seq 63; `commitBatchWithProof` seq 67; Rollup.sol:360-368. Every operation in it is checked: panics at :363 and :368.

So a successful commit made while a challenge is open stores `f = F + W + start` exactly, with `o = t`. When `t < start`, the extension `W + start − t` exceeds W.

Counterexample: commit batch 1 at t₁, challenge it at t₂ < t₁ + 40, then commit batch 2 at t₃ < t₂. That gives f₂ = 40 + 10 + t₂ > t₃ + 40 + g with g = 10. On M2 conjunct (c) prevented this, because there `t` is state and only `wait` raises it. M3 has no "now" to state (c) over.

**2. F and W are mutable.**
- `updateFinalizePeriodSeconds` and `updateProofWindow` (Rollup.sol:500-514, `onlyOwner`, any new non-zero value) write slots 152 and 153.
- `initialize` (Rollup.sol:160-186) also writes them. Its `initializer` guard `(!_initializing && _initialized < 1) || (!(len(code(this)) > 0) && _initialized == 1)` holds whenever the step's environment says `this` has no code. In s0 `_initialized == 1`, so **anyone can re-initialise in the model**. On chain they cannot.
- Lowering F after a commit breaks `f ≤ o + F + g`. Raising W during an open challenge breaks `inch → W ≤ g`.

**3. Mappings alias through the table.** A key missing from `kh` reads and writes slots `0 + i`.
- Two missing batch keys share slots 0..3 (slot 0 is `_initialized`).
- challengeState's loop then adds W twice to the same word, while `g` grows by W once.
- A present key whose table value lies within 3 of another entry overlaps that entry's struct.

**4. The deployment's hashed slots (global form only).** s0 holds nine hashed slots with real keccak values (model:13880).
- A table that maps some key to `σ − 1`, where σ is batch 0's originTimestamp slot, makes the global invariant false at s0. That key's `finalizeTimestamp` would then read 1700000010 while its `originTimestamp` reads 0.

**5. Word arithmetic needs no overflow premise.** The effects that matter are checked, and a failing check reverts:
- the loop's `f_i + W` (`w_add_ok`, Rollup.sol:491, panic 0x11; model `s_challengeState_5`, model:2656);
- the loop counter (`LFBI + 1 + it`, Rollup.sol:489, model:2620);
- the commit's `W + start`, `(W + start) − t`, `t + F` and the outer `+` (Rollup.sol:363, 368).

On every success path the stored values are therefore the exact Nat sums. This follows from the success of the step (the convoy equations of those checks) plus the Word↔Nat lemmas. It needs **no premise**: not 2^64 timestamps, not 2^256 sums.

The statement itself must read words as Nat, `N(w) = Σ to_nat(limbᵢ)·2^{32i}`, and add in Nat. Otherwise `o + F + g` could wrap. The model's own `n_of_w` reads the low limb only (model:702), so `N` is a new statement helper. The ghost is a Nat sum of `N(W)`.

### 1.3 The honest L_weak on M3: two forms

**G (global, as BUILD_PLAN I33 and whitepaper §5.9 state it).**

Binders: `p: Trace`, `k: Word`, `keccak256: Fn<Bytes,Word>`, over `run(p, s0)`.

Ghosts (IR 0.3 ghost terms, typed):
- `g = Σ N(W_pre)` over successful `challengeState` steps;
- `gP = Σ 1` over successful `initialize`, `updateFinalizePeriodSeconds` and `updateProofWindow` steps;
- `gT = Σ [inChallenge ∧ challenges[batchChallenged].startTime > block.timestamp]` over successful commit steps (`commitBatch`, `commitState`, `commitBatchWithProof`).

These need either several distinct ghost terms in one law (`trace_bound` refuses that: trace_bound.py:228) or a ghost over a list of actions (an additive IR change).

Premises:
- `sep(keccak256)`: entries pairwise ≥ 4 apart, every entry in [256, 2^256 − 4], keys distinct;
- `dep(keccak256)`: the table maps the nine deployment preimages to their real slots;
- `gP = 0`;
- `gT = 0`.

Claim:
- (a) `present(keccak256, batchDataStore, k) → N(f_k) ≤ N(o_k) + N(F) + g`;
- (b) `inChallenge → N(W) ≤ g`.

(b) is the view's (b). The view's (c) is replaced by the `gT = 0` premise, which is the only time fact the induction uses: `start ≤ t` at a commit made inside a challenge.

Each premise is necessary (§1.2 gives the counterexample), and each is true on chain. Disclose them anyway: `gT = 0` is exactly the fact the proof needs.

**L (local: the residue of one cancellation; the I64 C_t64_ext shape).**

Binders: `s: Reach`, `x: Act`, `y: Act`, `k: Word`, `keccak256: Fn<Bytes,Word>`.

Premises:
- `sep(keccak256)`;
- `present(keccak256, batchDataStore, k)`;
- x is a `challengeState` that succeeds from s;
- y is a `setPause` that succeeds from `s1 = step(x, s)`. A successful y is necessarily `setPause(true)`, the cancel branch, because x leaves the contract unpaused.

Let `s2 = step(y, s1)`.

- **L1** (deadline only): `N(f_k@s2) ≤ N(f_k@s) + N(W@s)` and `N(o_k@s2) = N(o_k@s)`.
- **L2** = L1 ∧ `N(reward[c]@s2) ≤ N(reward[c]@s) + N(v)`, where c is x's caller, v is x's `msg.value` (the deposit), and `reward` is `batchChallengeReward`.

L needs no time or parameter premises, because F and W are read in s. It needs no `dep`, because s0 is not involved, and no ghost or trace induction. Reach is not used, but keeps the law over the scope law's own domain.

**Why L2 and not L1.** The scope law's live residue channels after `(x; y)`, compared with `y` alone (§2.3), are exactly two:
- the +W extensions (O12), observable through `batchInsideChallengeWindow`, `finalizeBatch` and `challengeState`'s window guard;
- the deposit refund, `batchChallengeReward[challenger] += challengeDeposit`. This is setPause's cancel-branch write, seq 8, `cond _status ∧ inChallenge` (Rollup.sol:578-579). It is observable through `claimReward`.

The other differences are dead or equal:
- `batchChallenged` is read only under `inChallenge`;
- `challenges[bi]` is deleted, and was zero in s by the guard at :479;
- `inChallenge` and `_paused` are equal in both runs.

Replayable witnesses need `msg.value ≥ 1`, because the binding's submitter stand-in returns `challengeDeposit() = 1` (replay/M1/solidity/HolonTraceReplay.t.sol:42; the typed M3 replays answer `ISubmitter.challengeDeposit` with 1 too, replay/M3/sorts/report.json). So the refund channel exists in every replayable witness. It needs only a one-commit prefix, so a BFS over prefixes meets it before a two-commit extension witness.

A deadline-only contract would then mark a record whose witness it does not bound. That makes this a **question for the human** (Q-A below).

**My recommendation.**
- Take **L2** for I33.
- Record G as a follow-up: `trace_bound3`, once the typed mirror handles every action. I34 and I35 need that machinery anyway.

If the human holds to BUILD_PLAN's G, the cost and the premises above are the price. The refund question remains, and G's natural refund conjunct is messy: proveState also credits rewards.

### 2.2 The catalog change: a new UNDO sub-form `cancel`

**U3′, the cancel restore.** a's write w is:
- a whole, fresh, final and unconditional write;
- of a register pinned before it (U2) to a literal c, with w writing a literal v ≠ c.

ā writes c back:
- whole and final in ā, outside a loop, at a matching key;
- under a condition in which every conjunct is either a parameter of ā (or its negation), or the register's reading "holds v" (bool: `R` for v = 1, `!R` for v = 0; else `R == v`).

**U4′, criterion `cond`.** Some conjunct is a register reading.

The two rules are computed separately from `analyse()`, in a new section of the analysis document and through a new producer. This way the `pair` and `residue` producers stay the same objects. I27, I31 and I26's `m3_catalog()` checks require exactly that ("holds every producer of 1.x as the same objects": test_undo.py:904, test_disch.py:767, test_fact.py:480). I27's `test_catalogs` asserts `CATALOGS["holon-rules/1.3"]["UNDO"] == undo.PRODUCERS` (test_undo.py:1304-1305). So `undo.PRODUCERS` keeps `{pair, residue}`, and a separate `CANCEL` producer joins only the new catalog.

**Computed, by the probe** `probe/undo_cancel_probe.py`, which runs the scratch copy of holon over the committed document:
- exactly **one** cancellation: `(challengeState, setPause)`.
  - Restore: `inChallenge`. challengeState `writes[3]` (`inChallenge = 1`, seq 21) is undone by setPause `writes[3]` (seq 10, value 0) under `["&&", ["param","_status"], ["storage","inChallenge"]]`.
  - Pin: `guards[3]`, Rollup.sol:475 `!inChallenge`. Criterion `cond`.
  - Residues (U5): `[batchChallenged, batchDataStore, challenges]`. Static class `live`.
- The 5 pairs, 2 rejected pairs and 3 residue bases do not change. In particular `(challengeState, proveState)` stays rejected: proveState's restore is unconditional, so U4′ does not apply.
- The I27 toys `toyundo.extracted.json` and `drop/` gain nothing. The planted mutant `mutant/rollup_mutant.extracted.json` gains the same cancellation. I27-P11 reads the mutant's pairs, rejected pairs and residues, so its test is unaffected if cancellations stay a separate key.

## 3. Can `trace_bound` prove it? What must be built (question 3)

### 3.1 Why I65's class does not apply as it is

**`TraceBound.parse` requires an M1/M2 machine** (trace_bound.py:178-246):
- `em.binders`: `p: Trace`, then Nat binders only;
- `ex_` helpers over `run(p, s0)`;
- `m.threaded` false;
- exactly one ghost term (:228).

**Its lemmas name the machine's own definitions:**
- `A.HSt{fields}`, `A.Dec_<a>`, `A.D_<a>_Ok/Rev`, `A.exec_<a>`, `A.decide_<a>`, `A.last_ok`, `A.step`/`run` over `HSt` (render, :335-412; `decision_lemma` :484; `chain_lemmas` :507);
- list builtins `nth_d`/`set_d`/`List.append`/`take`/`drop` (LIBRARY :1201);
- the target library's kindless builtins through the pointwise sidecar (`_GoFn`, :575).

**The M3 typed model has none of these:**
- the state is `GSt{st: HSt{List<KW>}, tags}` (model:13936), with `gh_step`/`run` over it and one table `kh`;
- each action is a CPS chain of `s_<a>_i`/`k_<a>_i` over a context record `X_<a>`, with writes to an overlay `ov` and the code in `cd` (e.g. model:2564-2990);
- the 8 loops are `lp_<a>_<n>(fuel, d, x)` with fuel 4097 (model:2690, :2720);
- words are 8 U32 limbs, and the checks are `w_add_ok`/`w_ge`;
- mapping slots are `h_get(kh, preimage) + offset`;
- packed fields are masked `w_or`/`w_and`.

`holon.gen.mproofs.GENERATORS` (mproofs.py:896-902) is run by `holon.mpipeline` for M1/M2 laws only. On M3, `holon run` dispatches to the typed runners (`__main__.py:96-148`).

**What transfers:**
- the architecture: an invariant with the ghost's running sum as a parameter; s0 by computation; one lemma per action, walking its guard chain with each guard's equation kept (the convoy); induction on the trace, generalised over state and sums;
- the Bool-atom splitting helpers;
- the Nat comparison rules (`_Carry.le`).

**What already exists on M3 to reuse:**
- `holon.disch.mirror`, a symbolic stepper over exactly these chains with `Branch(cond)` splits. It refuses loops (mirror.py:16, :242).
- `holon.disch.lemmas`: absurdity, Bool, `w_refl`/`u_refl`, `st_frame`, `kw_frame` (lemmas.py).
- `holon.disch.order`: `w_lt` as lexicographic order over limbs, transitivity.
- `holon.sorts.prelude3_lemmas`: the h_get first-match lemmas `lp_filt_con`/`lp_filt_nil`.
- The DISCH proof routes refuse non-literal slots and bit-field stores (disch/proofs.py:288, :443, :449). Those are exactly what O12 needs.

2. **Proof engineering on the typed model.**
   - Loops have fuel 4097, and the transport needs a loop invariant over the carried store `c1_1`.
   - Bend checking time on 8-limb terms, inside a 15k-line import.
   - Linearity bookkeeping (`+` markers), as the probe showed.
   - Mitigation: W1 is generic and already half-proved; DISCH's 3000-line typed proofs check in seconds.

I27's search found commit→revert, and the I30 replays include challengeState. Two chained commits plus a challenge within prefix depth 3 is plausible but untested. Risk: P3 ends UNKNOWN.

Lessons from the probes:
- `%e : P` replaces `b` with `a`, where `e : {a == b}` and `P` marks `b`. So to replace a subterm T of the goal by T′, use `e : {T′ == T}`.
- A reused binder or hypothesis needs `+`.
- A definition must be filled before use; break mutual recursion by passing the induction hypothesis as a parameter.
- There is no `.1` projection; destructure with `(s, k) = sk`.
