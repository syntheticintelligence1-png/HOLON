## Done

### a. The `catalog_rederivation` entry kind (`holon/statement_diff.py`)

**What the entry carries.** One entry per law:
- `kind: "catalog_rederivation"`;
- `law_id` and `milestone`;
- `old_catalog` and `new_catalog`;
- `old_hash` and `new_hash`;
- `derivation`: the law's `{rule, subform, basis}`;
- `old` and `new`: the two law files as the registry history holds them;
- `reason`.

**When it is accepted.** `_catalog_rederivation` accepts it unsigned only when every one of these holds, mechanically:
- `old` and `new` are holon-ir/0.3 IR of one law of the entry's milestone. Only a 0.3 hash covers the catalog
  version.
- `old` names `old_catalog`, `new` names `new_catalog`, the two differ, and both are versions in
  `holon.rules.CATALOGS`.
- **The two law files are identical with `rule_catalog_version` masked.** Every other key is compared as canonical
  JSON: statement (claim, binders, premises, scope), facts, facts_sha256, notes, rule, subform and milestone.
- **The law's producer is the same object in both catalogs.** That is `CATALOGS[old][rule][subform] is
  CATALOGS[new][rule][subform]`, so a rule change can never hide behind a version bump.
- **`holon derive` reproduces both sides on the tree.** Under `old_catalog` it gives exactly `old` with `old_hash`, and
  under `new_catalog` exactly `new` with `new_hash` (`holon.rules.compute(.., catalog=..)`). The two DERIVATION
  entries are equal apart from the statement hash, and their `(rule, subform, basis)` is the entry's.

**What the tie checks.** `covers()` ties a `catalog_rederivation` to a registry change only when the entry carries the
change's two registry hashes exactly. A change that names no hashes is never covered by one. Together with the
derive check, this means:
- the committed old hash is the old statement hashed with today's refs;
- the hash difference is the catalog version alone, never a ref.

**What it never covers:**
- a claim or metadata change, refused by the mask and by derive;
- a rule change, refused by the producer check;
- a binding change. `holon.bindings.entry_covers` accepts it for a rebinding only when the statement hash is the only
  thing that changed in the binding;
- a deletion, which only a signed weakening covers.

**The acceptance record** names the facts file's sha256 and `catalogs: [old, new]`. A committed line is verified
against its appending commit (F-M2-22), and the record must name the entry's two catalogs.

**Generator.** `python -m holon.statement_diff recatalog --milestone M [--append]` (`catalog_rederivation_entries`)
builds one entry for every statement-hash change of `laws/<M>/REGISTRY.json` that no accepted entry covers yet and
whose old and new law files differ only in `rule_catalog_version`:
- `registry_changes` covers every commit since the log was introduced, plus the working tree;
- each entry carries the two statements and hashes as the registry history holds them;
- `append` checks each entry before writing it;
- any other uncovered change is named and gets no entry.

**Run on M3.** `recatalog --milestone M3 --append` appended 80 lines, 0 rejected, with 0 other uncovered changes.
- Two runs from the same log append byte-identical lines (checked by restoring the log and appending again).
- `tests_m3/test_catalog_rederivation.py` checks that the tool, with the lines hidden, rebuilds exactly the committed
  lines in order, each with its acceptance record.

**Tamper probes**, every one rejected (and in the test):
- the new claim drops a premise;
- both sides drop the same premise (neither catalog derives it);
- notes edited;
- facts edited;
- one catalog on both sides;
- a mislabelled catalog;
- an unknown catalog;
- a wrong old or new hash;
- another basis element;
- another milestone;
- a 0.2 side;
- two law ids;
- a producer swapped in the new catalog.

**A3** (`holon audit regress`) now passes each change's two hashes to `covers()`. Over `8a1dbe7..HEAD` it reports the
80 M3 hash changes of `4085005`, each `covered_by` exactly one line.

### b. I24's `test_no_link_and_no_log_line`, amended

**Why it had to change.** The preregistered assertion `assertFalse([e for e in lines if new.milestone == "M3"])` cannot
hold once M3's catalog moves, because G2's tie needs one line per changed M3 law. The test's own docstring states its
purpose (P11): no earlier law is about the linked codec document, so `links` is `[]` and I24 appends no line.

**The amended test:**
- keeps `links == []` unchanged;
- forbids every link line naming an M3 law: a `cross_milestone` entry, or any entry pairing an M3 law with another law
  (another id or another milestone);
- forbids every line whose new statement is an M3 law under I24's catalog (`tests_m3/_i24.py` `CATALOG_VERSION`,
  holon-rules/1.1).

On I24's tree every M3 law carried holon-rules/1.1, so there it forbids exactly what the original forbade. It stops
forbidding only lines whose new statement is an M3 law under a later catalog, which belong to later keys and which G2
checks. The amendment line is in `spec/M3/amendments.jsonl`, and `holon audit prereg --milestone M3` lists the test as
amended.

### c. `holon bind` for typed records (`holon/bindings.py`)

**What a typed law is bound to.** A typed-SORT law (`holon.rules.sort` subforms realize, as_member, reinterpret) is
bound to:
- the typed model `gen/<m>/sorts/models/<model>.bend`, the link its records name (Q25: no contract change);
- its statement and counter-law as `holon.sorts.run3` writes them, through `holon.sorts.proofs3.law_file`. The law file
  imports the file behind the link, with one hash table per `Fn` binder of the IR.

**The rebinding.** The binding of the 80 PAR laws differed from a fresh derivation in the statement hash only. Their
cones, emitted statements, counter-laws and models were unchanged. `holon bind --milestone M3 --accept-change
"<reason>"`:

For each typed law, the bound statement equals the committed `gen/M3/laws/<id>/LAWS.bend` (comments removed). The three
typed laws are covered: I30's contract and the I2 binding contract bind every law of a milestone, and G2's binding
diff names an unbound law.

## Surprises

   and `holon/inhabit.py` are in two places:
   - the holon-ir/0.2 and 0.3 generator closures (`holon.records.generator_files`, through lazy imports that the AST
     walk follows);
   - I30's `holon.sorts.run3` closure.

   So every M1, M2 and M3 record's `generator_sha256` changed with F30.
   - **M1 and M2.** These were re-run with `holon run` (`be1c749`). Only `generator_sha256` and `seconds` change;
     gen/M1 and gen/M2 are byte-identical; summary.json and deps.json are unchanged.
   - **M3.** The 80 PAR records (I25's area) and I30's two typed records (A30's area) now carry a stale
     `generator_sha256`. M3's G1, which was not run, would name them (Not done).
   - **The merge.** A30 and I25 also edit closure files, so the merge needs one more M1/M2 re-run either way.
2. **The old statements are reproduced exactly.** On today's tree, `holon derive` under holon-rules/1.1 gives the 80
   pre-merge law files (`0f11f2b`) byte for byte as canonical JSON, with their old registry hashes. Its DERIVATION
   `laws` equal the pre-merge ones, and only `evidence_sha256` and `inputs` differ, because the merge changed the
   evidence set. Each catalog_rederivation line is therefore a full re-derivation under both catalogs, not a textual
   diff.
3. **I10-R1 fails at the base and still fails.** `tests_m2/test_regress.py::TestRegress::test_no_hash_changes_since_the_seed`
   (I10-R1: A3 over `8a1dbe7..HEAD` reports 0 hash changes) has failed since the merge `4085005`. A3 reports its 80 M3

     holon.gate's `g8_not_applicable` is None. G8 passes.
   - **G11V over the PAR laws** had not run since the merge. It passes: 38 premised PROVED laws inhabited and 3 premised
     REFUTED laws, in 67 s.

   line is verified by its acceptance record only.

   - M1's hand-written holon-ir/0.2 law `L_sort_remaining` names subform `realize` too, so holon.gate's G2 on M1 looked

   - `tests_m2/test_bindings` caught it: five tamper and rebind tests failed with FileNotFoundError.

   - The bindings of M0, M1, M2 and M3 all re-derive with no difference.
