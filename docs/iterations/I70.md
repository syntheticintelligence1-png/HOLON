## Surprises
- **The cutover harness's Rollup is not a VReach state.** The harness (L2) deploys the Rollup proxy at V10's
  implementation and initializes it there. The versioned layer's reachability (I28's `VReach`) starts at the deployed
  path's entry (V3) and reaches V10 only through the eight migrations, as I83's inhabitants do. The contract states the
  laws over the product of `VReach` and each registry model's reachability, and the sidecar's search takes its
  candidates from the corpus's states. Those states are not in `VReach`, so the preregistered search could not run as
  stated, even with a linked layer.
- **Catalog 1.13, not the expected 1.12.** I34 takes 1.12 (the lead's catalog note of 2 Nov). Under hash scheme 2 the
  bump moves no statement hash, so S3's "each change a catalog_rederivation line" writes none (the lead's note;
  `tests_m3/test_hash_scheme.py`). At the landing, I34's 1.12 block in `holon/rules/__init__.py` must come before
  I70's 1.13 block: 1.13's base is the newest version defined when its block runs.
- **`register` is the one construct EXT1 leaves.** It calls `abi.encodeCall(IL2Staking.addStaker, (StakerInfo{...}))`,
  a dynamic struct argument. The coordinator kept it as debt.
- **Unnamed parameters broke model3's argument records.** `verifySignature(address, uint64[], uint256, bytes)` gave
  four fields named `x`. EXT1 renames repeats by position, and only in its own models.
- **T3 is the control.** A batch committed at V11 and slashed at V11 moves 0.2 ether from the Submitter to the Rollup,
  so the vacuous slash is specific to the pre-cutover batch, as F10 says.
- **M2 found a model3 defect.** A deleted struct's `bytes` member is not cleared (`claimWithdrawal`). It predates EXT1
  and touches any model that deletes such a struct.
- **P2's debt clause cannot fail as written.** The test collects the corpus actions on the registry from step keys
  `member` and `action`, which the corpus's steps do not have (they hold `call` and `label`), so the set is empty and
  the clause passes vacuously. P2 fails on its refinement clause instead.
