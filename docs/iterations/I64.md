## What I64 shows, and what it does not

- **What it shows:** the path from contract to mark works end to end, decided by the existing generators and refuters: a contract file, its registry hash, its L_weak as an ordinary law, the run's decision, the mark, G10. A false contract, a stale one and a tampered one each fail G10, and the instrument fixed at the prereg agrees with holon in every case it can decide.
- **What it does not show:** anything about O12. The toy's `challenge` is unguarded, so the L_weak holds of every state, and `reach_invariant` closes it by computation. O12's bound `f_j <= commit_j + F + W * #challenges` needs a proof class for linear bounds over Reach that no generator has yet.
- **The witness is not the one the law's notes anticipated.** The notes expected a finalize between the two deadlines, for example from `commit(0)`, with `q = [finalize(5)]`. The refuter found a shorter one from the initial state instead: it stacks a second challenge, which only the unguarded toy allows. Both are valid.
