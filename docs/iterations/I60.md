## Done

- **Catalog review.** The reviewers' full outputs are in `docs/iterations/I60_catalog_review.json`. They were two fresh agents with no stake, one with a fairness lens and one with a soundness lens.
- **What was fixed and confirmed.**
  - The fairness reviewer confirmed that I22's blocking findings are fixed:
    - the stratum-A scope is independent of which laws read the code;
    - the predicted sets come from one mechanical rule;
    - the operator sites recompute exactly;
    - the 6 `tce` mutants (compiler-equivalent) compile to Rollup's original bytecode.
  - The fairness reviewer signed off.
- **Blocking (soundness).** The enc_sol replay harness, which every K1 witness and every K2(c) falsification of a PROVED M0 law passes through, is specified so that its inputs do not overlap the refuter's candidates.
  - **Why they do not overlap:**
    - It copies the `requires` at Rollup.sol:294-295, so the state roots must be nonzero.
    - It types `l1MessagePopped` as uint16, where the model has 8 bytes.
    - Of 64 candidates per law, none both executes and fits the code's types.
  - **Consequence.** Under either literal reading the kill rate is about 0, so the outcome would be decided by a build choice made after the prereg.
  - **The working reading** is I16's store-level binding, which agreed on 2107 of 2107 inputs. The fix pins it down and adds a preregistered harness gate with planted controls that runs before any mutant.

## Advisory findings the replacement must address

- **Structural classes ignore law premises.** A148, A162 and A165-A168, and probably A081, are labelled reachable but sit behind a premise or a baseline-REFUTED law, so the stated ceiling is overstated.
- **The ceiling arithmetic is not stated.** The catalog should say that exit (b) cannot be met unless roughly a third of stratum A is judged equivalent.
- **Two operator-set problems.**
  - Variant multiplicity correlates with killability, so a per-site rate is needed.
  - The duplicate rule misses same-statement duplicates across operators, for example A004 and A029.
- **Unstated limitations.**
  - Unmutated sites: the version byte, the Go comparison boundaries and the pointer bumps in the dataHash preimage.
  - Stratum boundaries: `batch_data.go` and `L1MessageQueueWithGasPriceOracle.sol`.
- **Error and K2 handling.**
  - The `not_K2` and `errors` clauses can erase a valid K1 kill.
  - K2(b) must read the checker's own verdict line, not just its exit code.
  - K2(c) cannot witness a mutant that shrinks a domain (A081).
- **Survivor classification.**
  - There are no survivor classes for replay disagreements or baseline findings.
  - The masked-obstruction test is blind behind L_par_hdr_v2.
- **Unstated preconditions.**
  - I21's exit is a dependency the instrument never states.
  - There is no precondition that the null-mutant baseline reproduce the recorded statuses.
- **Matching and contradiction rules.** The rule for contradicting an equivalence verdict needs reachability, and the two `bvh_at` site laws cannot be matched.
