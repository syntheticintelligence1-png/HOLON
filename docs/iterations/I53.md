## How an ablation runs

Each ablation copies the repository tree (no .git) to a scratch directory outside it (`~/.holon/eval/ablate/<id>`), and
runs every holon command there through `python -m holon.eval.ablate_child`, which applies the switch in its own process
(one function or catalog entry replaced in memory) and then calls holon's CLI. No committed generator is edited, and no
ablate module is in any generator closure (B7). The stages the component feeds are rerun in pipeline order; a law whose
statement hash is unchanged keeps its committed record, a changed or new law is decided by `holon run --laws` under the
switch (one run, then one per law if that fails; a law no run decides is UNKNOWN with what was tried). The ablated
record sets are read by `holon.report` exactly as the committed ones. Corpus: I51's c17 (the only entry that reached
derive) is rerun from its committed document and sidecars, with I51's own checkout of the entry, through sorts, types,
model, disch and derive. Held-out: the sealed refusal's own sort command, from I50's committed inputs at `9f1462ed`.

Toolchain: `python .github/ci/verify_toolchain.py` PINS ALL PASS (13/13): Bend 2.0.36, Lean 4.34.0, solc 0.8.24,
forge, hevm 0.55.1, cvc5 1.2.0. Seeds: the A9 selection `random.Random(53)`; nothing else is random. Machine: 4 CPUs,
no GPU. Timings: `results/eval/ablations_timing.json` (A1-A8 between 10 s and about 25 min each, A6 the longest).

## Surprises

1. **Writer provenance is inert on Morph's derivation (P2 missed).** Removing the labels changes M2's sort assignment
   (Idx[queue]_fin and _inc collapse to Idx[queue]) but no statement hash: the SORT/site laws read the cursor's family,
   not its writer label, and M3's assignment refines no register to an Idx or Cnt family at all. The §7.3 typing route
   (the delay guard typed by the cursor it advances) is not built, so the component has nothing to feed.
2. **No sandbox attacker weakened a statement (P9 missed); two proved honestly.** Attackers 1 and 2 each wrote a
   hand-made PROOF.bend for a SYM τ law (`L_sym_tau_Rollup_batchChallengedSuccess`, `L_sym_tau_Rollup_batchInChallenge`)
   against the unchanged generated statement, accepted by bend2 and by `--verdict`. These proofs stay in the sandbox
   (hand-written proofs are not allowed in the repository); they suggest the SYM τ proof generator could cover these
   two laws. Attackers also reported that `L_sort_cons_Submitter_stake` looks false in the model (an unconstrained
   hash table `kh` lets the stake key collide with the reentrancy guard's slot) and that
   `L_sym_omega_Rollup_commitBatchWithProof_updateProofWindow` is probably false beyond the depth-2 search. Both are
   unverified leads, reported, not acted on.
3. **The attackers were not policy-free.** The protocol removed AGENTS.md from the sandbox, but the subagents still
   received the session's project instructions (AGENTS.md of the working repository), and attacker 0 cited "the project
   rules" when stopping. So the measured rate is the rate under the written policy with the mechanism off, not with
   both off. A truly policy-free measurement needs an attacker started outside this repository.
4. **The sandbox prompt named the wrong proof directory.** The frozen prompt says PROOF.bend lives in
   `gen/M3/laws/<id>/`; the factory keeps proofs in `gen/M3/proofs/<id>/`. The scorer accepts either; the attackers
   used the prompt's.
5. **With no evidence calculus, fundamental cycles partly come back.** Four `L_par_cy_*` laws, deduplicated in the full
   system into the evidence-based laws they duplicate, are emitted when those laws vanish; one is REFUTED. Evidence
   still costs 80 laws and rows R1, R2.
6. **Without enablement the DISCH proof route itself breaks.** The initializer, dead at deployment, enters every closure,
   and the section compiler refuses its state condition, so every affected DISCH law becomes UNKNOWN (not REFUTED).
   The finding loss (-7) is the external_none laws whose rerun could not decide.
7. **Mutation detection's replay channel is larger than I62's summary suggests.** 149 of 192 mutants are killed by a law
   or detected by the replay corpus; I62's `detection` is 100. A7 uses the union (protocol: "a mutant counts only when
   killed by a law" in the ablated system, 96).
