## What was fixed at the prereg

**System.** The system is the sealed repository at the sealed commit
(`scroll-tech/scroll-contracts` `42de954b`, tag v4.0.0). The labelled file is `src/L1/rollup/ScrollChain.sol`. The
scope follows I52's rule unchanged: every non-test `.sol` in `src/L1/rollup/`, which is 12 files. Only Solidity is in
scope, so `go_fuzz` and `two_cycle` are not_applicable.

**Labels.**
- **There is no known defect.** Nothing of the upstream may be fetched beyond the pin, and the pin is the newest tag
  in I49's snapshot, so no fix commit exists to label from.
- **What the labels are instead.** They are I50's preregistered rule classes (I50-P2's DISCH, FACT, SORT, SYM and
  UNDO), instantiated as obligations on the contract's 13 state-moving actions. That gives 65 (rule, action) labels,
  O1-O65. The `O<n>` ids are forced by I52's schema; they name nothing of Morph.
- **Where they sit.** A label's anchors are the action's own source spans, as I49's document records them: the
  declaration, its modifiers and the internal code it reaches.
- **How they are made.** `tests_m4/_i52b.py` generates them from I49's committed document. They never come from a
  baseline's output.
- **What a detection means.** A detection is a baseline's claim, confirmed by the blinded adjudicator, that the
  action violates that rule's obligation. It is not a verified defect.

**Matching.**
- **Location.** A finding is located when one of its locations lies in the labelled file and meets an anchor span,
  under I52's 150-line rule.
- **Adjudication.** One fresh tool-less process judges each (baseline, action) group and answers per rule. It never
  sees the baseline, the label ids, HOLON's outputs or the I49/I50 files.

**Budgets.**
- **Primary: the lead's rule.** I52's `holon_budget.heldout` is "I50's recorded wall clock". I50 recorded none, so
  I applied the rule the way I52 applied `holon_budget.morph` to M3, which also recorded none: measure I50's route on
  a scratch copy before any baseline result. The route is `holon target derive` then `holon target run`, and
  `wall_s = 60 * ceil(H / 60)`.
- **Secondary.** A 3600 s budget, fixed in the prereg because the primary one was expected to be degenerate. Every
  file of it lives under `results/eval/heldout/secondary/`.

## The adversarial phase (the builder's own)

- **Inflate.**
  - The five labels of an action share its anchors, so one finding locates five labels. The adjudicator answers per
    rule, so it cannot confirm all five by default.
  - Contract-wide and file-wide elements (more than 150 lines) count as their first line only, so slither's pragma
    and contract-level findings locate nowhere.
  - In a scratch probe, nothing recorded, the adjudicator got two findings on `revertBatch`: a naming-convention
    finding and a planted claim of commit/revert residue. It rejected the first and confirmed the second under UNDO
    only. So it can say both no and yes, and it picks the rule.
- **Deflate.**
  - Of slither's 35 findings in the labelled file, 11 are not located. They are views (`isBatchFinalized`), state
    variable declarations and a view-only helper (`_decodeBoolFromFlag`): none is an action site.
  - Findings in the 11 unlabelled scope files are never located. hevm's L2GasPriceOracle counterexample is one.
  - halmos and echidna were checked for vacuity (see Surprises 2 and 3).
- **Matching can fail.** `TestInstruments.test_matching_can_fail`, frozen in the prereg, places synthetic findings
  off every anchor, in an unlabelled file and on a long span, and gets no match for any of them. A finding on an
  anchor gets exactly its action's five labels.
- **Fold-ins.** Two:
  - the `assemble --check` fix (Surprise 4);
  - re-running echidna and halmos on I52's newer head (Surprise 2).

## Surprises

1. **The primary budget is degenerate.** I50's route ends at a refusal after 0.3 s, so "equal budget" gives each
   baseline one minute. The rule is applied as written. See the first question below.
2. **echidna stopped early in its first runs.** At its default 50,000-call test limit it ran about 14 s per unit of a
   3600 s budget. I52 then fixed this (`4e6776d1`: the budget is the only stop, and each tool gets its own work
   dir). I re-ran echidna and halmos in both tiers under the merged runner. Echidna now uses about 515 s per unit and
   still finds nothing. The superseded runs are in the history (`bcc1352e` and the secondary commit).
3. **Threat to validity: the stock harness makes halmos, and most fuzzing, vacuous on upgradeable contracts.**
   - I52's harness rule deploys each contract with default arguments and never calls `initialize`. So
     `onlyOwner`, `initializer` and role-gated calls all revert.
   - halmos reports "all paths reverted" for 15 of 15 checks on ScrollChain, in about 1 s per unit.
   - echidna and medusa see almost only reverts.
   - The status is still `run`, because the tool ran as the protocol says. The stock mode itself is close to blind
     on this target. That is a property of I52's frozen harness rule, not changed here. Per the lead's ruling
     (Question 3 below), this is a **threat to validity**: the frozen rule stands.
4. **I52's `assemble --check` was inverted.** `common.write_or_check` returns True when a file *differs*, and
   `cmd_assemble` did `ok &= write_or_check(...)`. So identical files printed INCOMPLETE, and a changed file would
   have passed. I fixed it by negation and checked it both ways: identical gives OK, one tampered message gives
   INCOMPLETE. The fix is commit `f84226467f63` (`assemble --check` inverted). It applies to I52's Morph and corpus
   `--check` too.
5. **hevm ran out of memory.** On the ScrollChain and ScrollChainCommitmentVerifier units at the secondary budget,
   hevm was OOM-killed (12.6 GB RSS in the session's memory cgroup) at 73 s and 574 s. Those two units are cut short
   by a resource limit, not by finding nothing. The record says exit -9.
6. **The reviewer answers early and empty at both budgets.** It neither uses its budget nor reports a finding. In its
   3600 s answer it named two candidates and withheld both as apparently intentional:
   - `commitAndFinalizeBatch` (the permissionless enforced-mode path) skips the all-V1-messages-finalized check that
     `finalizeBundlePostEuclidV2` makes;
   - `initialEuclidBatchIndex` is never set in this version, while `ScrollChainCommitmentVerifier` relies on it.

   Neither is a finding, so neither is counted. They are recorded here only as what the reviewer said. The first one
   sits at a labelled action, so P6 hinged on the reviewer's own triage.
