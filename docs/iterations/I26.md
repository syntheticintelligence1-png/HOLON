### (3) The FACT section, the product program and the proofs

- **The section** (`holon/fact/section.py`, `gen/M3/fact/models/morph_rollup_extracted_fact.bend`).
  - It imports I30's typed model as `M` and never copies it.
  - It holds:
    - the premises (`fx_wf`, `fx_cf`, `fx_nz`, `fx_tabs`, the closed hypothesis `fx_hyp_1`);
    - the kernel claim `fx_k3(d, o1, o2, pt, c)` with its parts (`fx_ws_eq`, `fx_ok_<x>`, `fx_pt_<x>`, `fx_c_<x>`);
    - the generated chooser companion of `batchBlobVersionedHashes` (fresh write: the caller; literal 0: bottom; copy:
      unchanged);
    - `run_fact`, and `fact_<x>` / `fhit_<x>` for each law.
  - Above the ghost marker nothing names a companion.
- **The product program** (`holon.gen.mproofs.product`, `holon/fact/product.py`). Routes in order: `guard`, then
  `injective`, for kernel laws; `provenance` for attribution laws.
  - `guard` walks the action's step chain with I31's mirror (`holon.disch.mirror`) and proves one lemma per branch.
    Where a branch tests the blob list's first entry against zero, the guard lemma rewrites with the premise that
    the list is empty. For `commitState`, Rollup.sol:276 is that guard.
  - The proof has 890 terms; with the law's 37 that is 927, under the budget of 1000. bend2 and BendTT both accept it.
  - `injective` and `provenance` are named and refused, each with its reason: no generator is built for them.
- **Counter-laws and inhabitants by parts** (`holon/fact/proofs.py`). See Surprise 1. The law's function at the
  witness is proved from its parts:
  - one closed lemma per leaf of the split function's skeleton (`fk3_<x>` / `fh3_<x>` for kernel laws, `fa2_<x>` /
    `fh2_<x>` for attribution laws);
  - a lemma over variables (`sz`, or `sz1` and `sz2`) for the unfolding: evaluation stops at a variable, so each
    kernel conversion compares terms that are syntactically equal or stuck the same way;
  - for an attribution witness, `run_fact`'s state and companions after each trace step as literals, one step lemma
    each, rewritten in innermost first.
- The leaf values are the unique assignment the skeleton forces for the law's value.
  - When no assignment is unique, the closed lemma is used as before.
  - If both kernels reject the by-parts text, the closed text is tried.
  - Nothing here is chosen by a name: the split functions are the section's own, read from its text.

### (4) The searches

- **K1** (kernel laws, `FactSearch.kernel`): x1 is S1's step or a single-leaf variant that succeeds; x2 is a
  single-component variant of x1. The real side checks each model witness (`run3.Real`, the I20 step harness).
  - **The blob-hash list.** Its variants are I30's (the empty list and one-element lists of the pool's blob words) and
    every list of the law's measure domain (F9's admissible lists of the consumer). This is a design choice, written in
    the module's docstring and not in the sidecar (Question 4).
  - No candidate comes from the deployment's recorded calls. Round 2 offered bytes arguments the byte strings those
    calls carried (`hint_bytes`; on this binding `0x01`, the hand-written harness's proof token). The review removed it
    (Done (10), Surprise 2).
  - A witness refused in its first run skips the remaining x2 variants of that x1.
- **The attribution search** (`FactSearch.attribution`), as reworked after round 1 (`156dc4a`, `c820139`).
  - The prefixes are states of the product of the typed model and the chooser companions, as the contract says
    ("prefixes ... with the chooser companions"): a prefix is dropped only when its typed state, last time and
    companions (`run_fact`'s g) are a state already seen (round 3; rounds 1-2 dropped prefixes on the typed state and
    time alone).
  - The candidates y are the steps of w that take the pin's copy branch (`fx_pin_<x>` in y's pre-context), as the
    contract says ("y a candidate step of w taking the pin"). Only those count against the budget, and only those
    are run.
  - "The first (prefix, y) whose claim fails": a (prefix, y) whose claim fails on the model goes to the real side
    first. A y the deployment refuses costs no continuation search.
  - "Then t": shortest first, each length depth first. Its last step makes the consumer's call with A[k].m. The steps
    before it come in rounds over the actions: each action's i-th candidate in turn, the actions that write most of
    the families the consumer reads first. So every action's S1 step is tried before any variant.
  - A model continuation the real side refuses is counted in the bound, and the search goes on to the next.
  - The continuation budget, or the run's time box, ends the continuation (t = [], with the bound), never the witness.
  - Every state the search saves is forgotten once explored: the evaluator's saved state, and the state and pools that
    I30's refuter cached for it.
