## Surprises

1. **`L_fact_Z`'s consumer shows no divergence.**
   - Derivation rejects both equal-commitment batches: "blobs length can not be zero" vs a zstd decode error on the all-zero blob. It then logs the error and stops at that batch in both cases.
   - The implementation first counted the two different error strings as the obstruction. Two reviewers flagged that as overstating, and the rule now compares outcome classes (accepted, rejected, panic). So:
     - the consumer side is "not observed";
     - the bin is `pending`;
     - O22 is partial;
     - the law-level `obstruction_reproduced` is false, so `tests_m1/test_m1_replay.py` fails on `L_fact_Z`;
     - two preregistered I4 tests fail, as the record of this miss: `test_fact_z_consumer` and `test_predicted_bins_new_replays`.
   - The law itself stays REFUTED and replayed on chain.
2. **The `L_tag_BVH` consumer half adds little.** Go reads the committed headers correctly. The "observation" (aggregate ≠ field) is keccak(x) ≠ x, true by construction once the chain shows the v1 hash in the v2 header. The only Morph code that compares a v2 field with an aggregate (`validateLegacyTransitionRecord`) runs only for locally sealed records. A real consumer replay needs a real KZG blob; the harness mocks `blobhash`.
3. **O2 is partly stale at the pinned commit.**
   - At 3e44e79 the Go batch producer builds version-2 headers by patching byte 0 of `BatchHeaderV1.Bytes()` (`common/batch/batch_cache.go:1727-1731`). So O2's "no v2 encoder" no longer holds there.
   - `L_par_hdr_v2` refutes the frozen M0 law against `BatchHeaderV1.Bytes` only. The coverage rule still says full; the table carries a note.
4. **The review confirmed 24 of 28 findings: 2 high, the rest medium or low.** The two high ones were consumer sides keyed on the unhashed `subform` and chain contradictions binned as `pending`. All confirmed findings are fixed; the G6 bypasses below are pinned by `tests_m2/test_i4_review.py`:
   - consumer sides keyed on the unhashed `subform`;
   - provenance trusted from the report;
   - flags never checked against the evidence;
   - chain contradictions binned as `pending` instead of model defects.
5. **Sidecar and amendments after the prereg.**
   - `spec/ledger/maps_to.json` now maps `L_par_hdr_ssvh` to D1, as its own note always said it bears on D1. D1 drops to partial. One reviewer flagged the exclusion; the verifier did not count it as a defect. I took the conservative reading.
   - Amendments logged in `spec/M2/amendments.jsonl`:
     - I1's pending-field test (`witness_provenance` and `filter_bin` are no longer pending);
     - I1's G6 label test, amended twice (the first edit was not internally consistent);
