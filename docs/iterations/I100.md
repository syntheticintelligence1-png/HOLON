## The rule (`holon/rules/par_record.py`, catalog holon-rules/1.16)

**Instances.** One per tuple:
- a linked codec document;
- a carrier of kind bytes;
- an encoder into it with a versions list;
- a version N;
- a getter family: the decoders of the document from the carrier that share one source qualifier, the text before the
  last ".", which is `(pkg.T)` for a Go method.

**Components.** Each family member active at N gets one, from the encoder's layout and the member's read range, never
from laws:
- a field, whose bytes 0..w-1 the layout places there in order (rule X7's inverse atom);
- a constant, when every cell there is a constant;
- otherwise none, and the member is missing.

**`constant_read`** gives one law per constant component, `L_par_rt_<e>_<d>_v<N>`: `d(e(h)) == some(c)` under P1's
version premise.

**`record_round_trip`** gives, for an instance with no missing member, `L_par_rec_<e>_<F>_v<N>`: the conjunction of
the members' components, under the same premise and nothing else. An instance with a missing member gives no law. It
is an obstruction (`missing_component`) in `gen/<M>/par_record/basis.json`, never a dropped law.

The catalog keeps holon-rules/1.15's producers (the same objects) and adds the two sub-forms. derive's `par_record`
part runs them. Their names are disjoint from `holon.rules.par`'s.

## Deciding a conjunction (`holon/gen/linked.py`, `holon/codecrun.py`, `holon/bindings.py`)

- The emitter states a conjunction as an exs telescope.
- `open_refl` opens the record and gives one reflexivity per conjunct.
- The refutation search returns the first conjunct a fill separates. The counter-law states that conjunct's negation.
- The record keeps its index as `failing_conjunct`. The binding holds one counter-law sha256 per conjunct
  (`counter_binding`, as for I7's conjunctions).
- Statements with one equation emit byte for byte as before.
- B4 checks a planted wrong constant. A copy of the v1 record law whose `SequencerSetVerifyHash` conjunct claims 32
  bytes of 0x01 is REFUTED on conjunct 10, and Bend and BendTT accept the counter-law.

## The back-test's record map (`holon/backtest.py`)

**The record map.** `spec/M3/lib/backtest_record_map.json` maps the hand decoder `dec_go` and its projection `view`,
slot by slot, to the component getters. Each slot's getter is the method its getter code names; the
`blobVersionedHash` slot gets `BlobCommitHash`. holon backtest reads the map beside B1's and never recomputes it.

**Pairing (B2 amended).** A conjunction over one record binder collapses when:
- every conjunct is `g(x) == some(t)`, with one `x` for all;
- every `g` is a member of the map's family, and at least one is a slot's getter.

Then:
- each slot conjunct's `t` must be the slot's image: v's field under B1's field map, or a constant where the field map
  gives the slot no field, which adds the hand premise `slot = c`;
- the family's other conjuncts are dropped;
- the claim becomes `dec_go(x) == some(view(v))`, and the rest is B2.

**Bridges (B3 amended), under the M0 law's binders and pinned premises:**
- `dec_go(a) == pack_X(a)`, with `a` the M0 law's argument of `dec_go` on the hand model;
- `view(h) == tup_X(mv(h))`.

Each is decided by `open_refl`. Failing that, a counter-law at the first hand fill (zeros, ones, counting, 0xff; pins
applied) where the compiled models disagree. Failing that, UNKNOWN. A stale component model is refused.

**Why acceptance stays sound when slots are missing.** The bridges range over the matched slots X. So the record law
plus the PROVED bridges implies the M0 law. When a slot of the hand decoder is missing from X, the bridges themselves
differ and are refuted.

## Surprises

- **A source without the constant slot's premise does not pair.** The candidate's basis says a bridge "is refuted
  where the source lacks that premise". Here the collapse adds the hand premise `slot = c` for a constant slot, so
  such a source fails alpha-equivalence and gives no pairing, a rule gap instead of a refuted bridge. M0's
  `L_rt_go_vN` all have the premise, so no outcome depends on this.
- **Pairing is lenient on missing slots by design.** That is how m06 is caught. A record law with fewer slots than
  the hand decoder still pairs, and its bridges are refuted. Acceptance stays sound because the bridges range over
  the matched slots.
- **Lean, the second kernel, has no proof for a conjunction.** `holon.gen.lean_linked` states it with ∧, but its
  `open_refl` emitter covers equations only ("open_refl for a claim that is no equation"). So the six record laws are
  I1-shared, with lean4 not_run `unsupported_form`, an allowed reason. The six read-backs are I2.
- **The re-make re-made only the 12 new laws.** `holon land dirty` lists 204 dirty laws at the merge base. The other
  192 are dirty by trace at the lead too: their inputs changed after their traces, such as `gen/M3/evidence.json` at
  I93 and `holon/bend.py`. I set the land state's dirty set to the 12 new laws before `holon land plan`.
- **Two `tests_m3/test_derive_par.py` tests were already red at the lead,** both because I93's Go sub-forms and laws
  are excluded nowhere:
  - `TestRegistry.test_catalogs`;
  - `TestDeriveM3.test_derivation_follows_the_rules`, which reports 5 extra laws, the gox ones.

  I100's licensed filter keeps its own 12 out, so they fail exactly as before. Neither test is in CI.
- **The re-make ran the back-test after the regen's report step,** so `holon land check` flagged the M3 report's pin
  of the table. I regenerated the report (`dbecea26`).
