### The theory core, as fixed text (`holon/gen/theory.py`)

- **`gen/M3/theory/core/LAWS.bend`** is the frozen text: 19 laws over per-step bundles (`Cons`, `LInv`, `Pres`, `Onto`, `WalkOk`).
- **`gen/M3/theory/core/PROOF.bend`** proves all 19 by hand, once (gap T-G7).
  - The proofs go by induction on the walk. Each bundle is matched step by step and consumed once. Rewrites are packaged as top-level casts (`cast_x`, `cast_w`), so a computed proof can be passed as an argument.
  - Double reversal (`rev_go_rev`, `rev_rev`) and the reverse run (`rev_run`) are proved generically.
  - Lemma 6.2's four laws are closures over `Valid`. Membership is `Data`, so `+r` serves both sides of a transitivity.
  - Ex falso goes through a motive `bool_motive(b, P)`.
- **`gen/M3/theory/lean/Theory.lean`** is the frozen definitions block followed by the 9 frozen theorems with Lean 4 proofs.
  - The proofs use 15 helpers: the walk algebra, one step seen through its edge, bijectivity along a step and along a walk, transport, and `flat_core`.
  - After the build review it also has two theorems that are not frozen statements: `forward_transport` and `forward_potential_parallel`. They state, in Lean, the form every instance applies (Thm 6.4 (ii) in potential form over forward walks, with no inverse).
  - `holon.lean.check_text` returns `checked`. The axioms are `propext` and `Quot.sound` only: `lemma_6_2` uses none, the `thm_6_3_*` theorems use `propext`, and the rest use both.
- **No target is named.** No module of the generator closures imports these three modules (gap T-G5).

### Basis sufficiency by application (`holon/gen/basis.py`, `holon/gen/congruence.py`, `python -m holon basis`)

- **Inputs (rule T0).**
  - The linked codec documents and diagrams are refused (exit 2, named) unless they are byte for byte what `holon codecs` and `holon diagram` write now.
  - Each used law's `LAWS.bend` must carry the registry's `statement_hash`.
  - Each used law's record must name the instance's model and carry its sha256. That second check came with the build review.
- **Rules T3-T6.** Each instance has its graph, its BFS tree and its available equations (the PROVED laws). Each non-tree edge has a derivation of its fundamental cycle by prefix rewriting, at most 6 rewrites. Each instance also has its certificates.
- **Rule T7: the proof by application.** Each instance `PROOF.bend` (about 790 lines) has these parts:
  - **Bridges** (`bridge_*`): each opens the root record once and proves by `{==}` either an equation between two pins of one record (`A0.pin_h(A0.pin_h(h)) == A0.pin_h(h)`; an applied law's version pin on `sigma(pin_h(h))`) or a pinned field's constant. These are the only defs that write a model record.
  - **`avail_<i>`:** the PROVED law of equation i, applied at the instance's argument, rewritten through its bridge and lifted into the carrier sum with `Equal.cong`.
  - **`edge_<k>`, one per edge:**
    - a tree edge is proved by `{==}`;
    - a non-tree edge is proved by the derivation `holon.gen.congruence` emits: `Equal.trans` over the steps, `Equal.sym` for a right-to-left step, and `Equal.cong` whiskering each step by the remaining suffix.
  - **`step_fact`:** a valid step respects the potentials. For each forward edge k, it rewrites `u` to edge k's source with `core_nat_eq_of_is_eq` and applies `edge_k`. Every invalid step (a backward step, or a missing edge) is closed by `core_efq_bool`.
  - **`cons_of`:** builds a walk's `Cons` bundle from its `WalkOk` evidence.
  - **The instance law** is one application of `core_parallel_agree`.
  - **Each certificate** is the instance law at two concrete walks, read through `un3`, with the constant reads rewritten by their getter bridges.
- **Rule T8b: the semantic canary.**
  - The witness is the first candidate on which the model's two walks differ. The candidates are, per field the root law pins on h, the zero record with that field's first byte moved off its constant.
  - `holon.bend.run` evaluates the candidates on the compiled model, with the fingerprinted checker and a sanitized environment (a build-review fix). No oracle is involved.
  - The proof is the counter-law of BEND_PRIMER 5.4, through an untrusted `car_eq` and the motive `holds`.
- **Rule T9.** The basis document `gen/M3/theory/basis/header_length.json` is written. `--kernels` writes `report.json`, binding each proof file's sha256 to bend, `--verdict` and Lean.
  - The build review added two keys to the report: `statements` (the `LAWS.bend` sha256s) and `independence` (`I1-shared` for each instance; see M4 below).
  - A proof file counts as checked only if it fills every expected law: ALL PROOFS CHECK on an empty file proves nothing.
- **CLI behaviour.** `holon basis` owns only `core/`, `lean/`, `basis/` and `report.json` (the last only with `--kernels`). Exit codes are 0 written, 1 `--check` differs, 2 refused (`holon basis: refused:`), 3 construct refused. Nothing is written unless the whole build succeeds.

## The build review (folded in the commit after `8a4222b`)

An independent reviewer attacked the build (xhigh effort). It ran 23 core mutants, all 70 non-tree edges replaced by `{==}` one by one, 15 targeted instance mutants and 3 helper mutants of the canary. Every mutant was rejected at the mutated def. It found nothing blocking. Its findings and what was done:

- **M1. Rule T2's text checks could be bypassed in Lean.**
  - **How.** A def named with an escaped identifier holding a block-comment opener (`def «/-»` … `def «-/»`) hides the code between from `tests_m3/_i42.py::lean_code` and from `holon.lean.strip_comments`. The demo hid a `macro_rules` that rewrites `=`. Lean then accepted `cor_6_5_potentials` elaborated as `… → True`, while its text still equalled the frozen statement.
  - **Fixed in the contract.** `TestLean.test_lean_definitions_and_statements_are_frozen` now refuses any escaped identifier after the marker and applies `holon.lean.FORBIDDEN` to the contract's own comment-free reading. This is an amendment, recorded in `spec/M3/amendments.jsonl`.
  - **A new check.** The new `test_lean_elaborated_types_are_the_frozen_ones` compares each frozen theorem's elaborated type under `pp.all` with the type elaborated from the frozen statements. Both checks reject the reviewer's cheat file. The committed file never had an escaped identifier.
  - **Not fixed here.** `holon.lean.strip_comments` has the same hole, so Lean G3 can be bypassed in every milestone. `holon/lean.py` is in the holon-ir/0.2 and 0.3 generator closures, so changing it would change every M1-M3 record's `generator_sha256`. It is Question 5.
- **M2. Rule T7's text checks could be bypassed in Bend.**
  - **How.** They strip `#…` per line regardless of string literals. A record pattern after a `"#"` string in a one-line match was invisible, and dead `_a = avail_0(h)` lines satisfied the naming check.
  - **Fixed in the contract.** An instance or canary `PROOF.bend` may now hold no string or char literal, so `#` always starts a comment. Each `bridge_*` body must be exactly `match h:`, one record case, `{==}`. Both are amendments.
  - **The generator.** The generator's own comments no longer use apostrophes. The reviewer's cheat file is rejected.
- **M3. The witness search ran `bend` from PATH with an unsanitized environment.** The search had no `BEND_NO_TELEMETRY` (so bend's daily version check made a network GET) and passed `BUN_OPTIONS` through. It now runs through `holon.bend.run`. Soundness was never at stake, because `--kernels` re-checks everything through `holon.bend`.
- **M4. The Lean core covers nothing an instance applies.**

## Surprises

1. **The prereg review found that P2 could never pass.** `holon.lean.check_text` audits each theorem with `#print axioms <bare name>`, and the frozen Lean text declared its theorems inside `namespace HolonTheory`. The namespace was removed, and `autoImplicit` is off, before any build.
2. **The prereg review strengthened the contract.** It added command rules after the Lean marker; a localizing Thm 6.3 (`thm_6_3_local` now names the step that leaves R, using a decidability hypothesis); T7's checks of "by application"; the semantic canary as P4; the construction canary, the document and the certificates moved to regressions; record inputs by status; and R5 restricted to the gate checks that pass at the prereg.
3. **One error before the Bend core checked.** A type-level def cannot return an erased parameter (`expected : -P`, `observed : P` for `bool_motive(b, -P)`), so P is now live. After that, all 19 laws, the three instances and the three canaries were accepted by both kernels on the first run.
4. **Lean: `beq_self_eq_true` pulls in `Classical.choice` for Nat.** The proofs use `beq_iff_eq` instead. `thm_6_4_i_ii` never uses its spanning-tree hypothesis (a linter warning): (i) ⇒ (ii) needs only bijectivity.
5. **70 of the 73 available laws are needed.** `enc_sol`'s round trips of `BlobVersionedHash` at v0 and v1 and of `BlobHashesHash` at v2 appear in no shortest derivation. Each reads the second of two decoders on one slot (a PAR-weak pair's).
6. **Same file, two aliases: one module.** The instance proofs import each law's `LAWS.bend` under an alias of their own, while the instance `LAWS.bend` imports the root law's as `L0`. Bend resolves both to one module, so `A0.pin_h` and `L0.pin_h` are the same def. A probe checked this before the build.
7. **The build review's two bypasses** (M1 and M2 above) were in the preregistered instrument's checks, not in the committed files. The fixes are three amendments, each a strictly stronger check.
8. **`holon.lean.strip_comments` misreads `«/-»` as a comment** (M1). The hole is wider than I42: Lean G3 can be bypassed in every milestone. It is reported, not fixed here (Question 5).
9. **The canary is narrower than P4's sentence.** The witness moves the one field the root law pins on h (`SequencerSetVerifyHash`). The k-side version pin is part of `sigma` and is never tested. The canary amounts to I24's already REFUTED full cross-component law restricted to `k = pin_k(zero, h)`. P4 passes as worded: the instance law needs its h-side pins.
10. **The Lean core does not apply to the instances** (M4). Its Thm 6.4 statements need an invertible fragment, and the header's edges are not invertible. The instances apply the potential form over forward walks, now stated in Lean too (`forward_potential_parallel`). The instances themselves remain checked by Bend's two kernels only (I1-shared).
