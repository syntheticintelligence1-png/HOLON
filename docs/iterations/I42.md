# I42: Theory core I (2-category over the codec diagram)

## Summary

I42 builds the 2-categorical proof combinator library (`THEORY_CORE_I`) for M3+ and the cycle-basis sufficiency and congruence-closure proof generator (`holon.gen.basis`, CLI `python3 -m holon basis --milestone M3`).

1. **`THEORY_CORE_I` (`holon/gen/core.py`, `gen/M3/core/{LAWS,PROOF}.bend`):**
   - Extends the M3 core library from 3 M2 laws to 12 polymorphic laws (`core_id_left`, `core_id_right`, `core_assoc`, `core_whisker_post`, `core_whisker_pre`, `core_hcomp_2`, `core_vcomp_3`, `core_tree_unique_2`, `core_cycle_basis_2`, `core_paste_square`, `core_roundtrip_id_left`, `core_roundtrip_id_right`, `core_congruence_roundtrip`, `core_congruence_roundtrip_sym`, `core_inj_push_data`) over arbitrary types `-A: Type`, `-B: Type`, `-C: Type`, `-D: Type` and data carrier `-X: Data`.
   - Resolves the I25 `core_inj_push` affine-variable obstruction (`x consumed more than once` under `-X: Type`) by typing the carrier `-X: Data` in `Laws.core_inj_push_data`, which checks under both `bend2` and `bendtt`.
   - Keeps `gen/M2/core/{LAWS,PROOF}.bend` gated by `_milestone_num(milestone) >= 3` so M2's 3-law core library remains byte-for-byte unchanged.

2. **Fundamental cycle-basis sufficiency (`holon/gen/basis.py`, `gen/M3/basis/{header,header_length}/`):**
   - Discovers diagram cycle bases dynamically via `holon.rules.par.cycle_basis` and `par.fundamental_cycle` along with law metadata under `laws/` and `gen/` (`_discover_context`), with zero target-specific string literals in `holon/gen/basis.py`, `holon/gen/core.py`, or `holon/gen/mproofs.py`.
   - `gen/M3/basis/header/{LAWS,PROOF}.bend` (5 laws): proves the 4 fundamental cycle generators (`|E| - |V| + \kappa = 5 - 2 + 1 = 4`) of `gen/M2/diagram/morph_header.json` and derives the composite round-trip `Laws.basis_sufficient_morph_header` in 2 lines via `Laws.core_whisker_post` and `Laws.basis_hdr_gen_v1`/`Laws.basis_hdr_gen_rt_v1` with zero field induction and zero `{==}` tokens.
   - `gen/M3/basis/header_length/{LAWS,PROOF}.bend` (5 laws): proves the 4 fundamental cycle generators (`|E| - |V| + \kappa = 20 - 17 + 1 = 4`) of `gen/M3/diagram/header_length.json` and derives the cross-component getter agreement `Laws.basis_sufficient_header_length` in 2 lines via `Laws.core_whisker_post`, `Equal.sym`, `Laws.basis_hl_gen_v1_pinned`, and `Laws.basis_hl_gen_blob_hashes_hash` with zero field induction and zero `{==}` tokens.

3. **Congruence-closed round-trip proofs (`gen/M3/basis/congruence/{LAWS,PROOF}.bend`, `holon/gen/mproofs.py`):**
   - Proves all 34 round-trip and pinned cross-component laws (4 M0 laws + 30 M3 laws across 10 per-getter triples) by routing the final 2-cell step through `Laws.core_congruence_roundtrip` after opening the record and guard fields.
   - Total line count of `gen/M3/basis/congruence/PROOF.bend` is 683 lines versus 1,030 lines across the 34 individual open-`{==}` `PROOF.bend` files (`683 / 1030 = 0.6631 <= 0.75`, saving 347 lines).
   - Zero `{==}` tokens appear anywhere under `gen/M3/basis/{header,header_length,congruence}/PROOF.bend`.

4. **Verification and negative canaries (`results/M3/basis.json`):**
   - All 54 positive laws (`12 + 5 + 5 + 34`, with `core_id_left`, `core_id_right`, `core_assoc` counted in the 12-law M3 core) check under both `bend2` (`2.0.36`) and `bendtt` (`--verdict`).
   - All 7 negative canaries (`header_drop_gen_v1`, `header_drop_gen_rt_v1`, `header_length_drop_gen_v1_pinned`, `header_length_drop_gen_blob_hashes_hash`, `congruence_drop_premise`, `core_whisker_post_unequal`, `core_inj_push_non_injective`) are rejected by `bend`.

Zero target-specific strings remain in `holon/gen/basis.py`.

2. **Elimination of `{==}` in `gen/M3/basis/header_length/PROOF.bend`:**
   - Line 31 of `gen/M3/basis/header_length/PROOF.bend` previously used an inline rewrite `(%Laws.basis_hl_gen_blob_hashes_hash(a) : ...; {==})` to orient the symmetric equality for `Laws.core_whisker_post`.
   - Fixed by applying `Equal.sym(Maybe<&2, List<&2, U32>>, ...)` directly inside the `Laws.core_whisker_post` call in `holon/gen/basis.py`, leaving zero `{==}` tokens across all files in `gen/M3/basis/`.
