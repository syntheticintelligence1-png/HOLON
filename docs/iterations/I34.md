# I34: rule SYM on M3, the typed extracted Rollup

I34 states rule SYM's laws on M3, the typed model of the extracted Rollup: τ over the Time words, π over the set roles,
ω over the eligible pairs of actions, and the Rollup's Time sentinel. ι and μ are vacuous: no M3 model has an Idx or
Amt sort. Splits are promoted only by route (i) or route (ii). O21 and the messenger's sentinels moved to I34b (I92).

**Outcome.** The 77 laws were derived under holon-rules/1.12 and decided on the merged tree at the landing.

| sub-form | laws | PROVED | REFUTED | UNKNOWN |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| τ (time translation) | 13 | 0 | 4 | 9 |
| π (principal permutation) | 11 | 0 | 3 | 8 |
| ω (reordering) | 49 | 0 | 6 | 43 |
| sentinel | 4 | 4 | 0 | 0 |

- Every REFUTED law's counter-law is accepted by bend (ALL PROOFS CHECK) and BendTT, and its witness is replayed in two
  worlds on the real Rollup.
- Every REFUTED τ or π record carries filter_bin `split_pending`. Each split has its adjudication request with both
  hypotheses: `audit/sym_adjudications/` holds tau_time, pi_submitter and pi_challenger.
- No law is PROVED by an equivariance proof. The π and τ proof generators need a relational mirror of an action's
  statement chain over the structural store, which is not built. So the laws that are not refuted stay UNKNOWN, with
  their search bounds named.

## Surprises

1. **τ breaks at resets as well as at comparisons.**
   - The localizer named the action's only Time-against-constant comparison whenever the two runs' check codes agreed.
     With equal codes every guard passed alike, so no comparison crossed. The dry run's finalizeBatch record named
     `:630`, a guard both runs passed.
   - It now returns `(site, kind)`:
     - "compare": the failing guard when the codes differ, or a view's return;
     - "reset": the action's write of a constant into a Time word (holon.sym.time_resets): one write site, or the one
       that resets a sentinel register when several do.
   - J7 calls a reset to the sentinel the intended absent value, with no law. The reset still breaks τ, which J7's
     comparison sites do not cover (P5).
2. **holon.bindings had no case for a SYM law.**
   - G2's binding check and `holon bind` compute every M3 law's binding. A SYM law fell through to the untyped model path
     `gen/M3/model/<model>.bend`, so both crashed.
   - A SYM law now binds over the typed model and its sym section, as an UNDO law binds over its trace section. Its
     emitter (holon.symd.proofs, imported by name) reproduces the decider's law files byte for byte.
3. **decided_by read one attempt shape.**
   - holon.symd.run writes holon.undo.run's shape: kind counter, the checker's exit code under `checker`.
   - The test's helper read only the codec pipelines' shape: kind counter-law, exit_code on top. So O19's checked record
     read as undecided.
   - Amended (spec/M3/amendments/I34.jsonl, instrument_inconsistency). It still requires a checker that exited 0.
4. **J4's "the bump moves every M3 statement hash" was written under hash scheme 1.** PV2A's scheme 2 leaves the catalog
   version out of the hash, so the bump to 1.12 moved none.
