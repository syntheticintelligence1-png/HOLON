# I40: refutation engines, Appendix D encodings, BMC over the extracted TRANS edges, the final filter

Plan: `docs/BUILD_PLAN.md` §4 I40. Prompt: `prompts/I40_refute.md`. Contract: the docstring of `tests_m3/test_refute.py`.
Key: `I40` in `spec/M3/predictions.json` (prereg `62fb13d`). Base `fbd14d3` (A30, F31-F33, I27 UNDO, CI). Branch `i40`.
Nothing is pushed to the integration line; a CI validation branch is listed under Checks. No line was appended to
`spec/M3/amendments.jsonl` or `spec/M2/amendments.jsonl`: the two I4 amendment lines the prereg expects (R3) are
needed only when the dom_v0_long and eta2_state targets come back no_path, and neither did (see P7).

**Summary.** The engines exist and run on the real targets: pinned z3 4.16.0 with the Appendix D encodings (E1 lazy,
ground), layout queries, a codec engine over both codec documents (all 88 codec laws agree with their statuses), the
fuzzer over linked components, BMC over the extracted TRANS edges with replay of every path on the real Rollup through
the binding's harness, emitted Foundry and Go tests, rule K1-K4 concretization and the final filter F1-F4 with G6 and
G1 over it. The planted fixture (P9) behaves exactly as predicted. Rule K3 (a commit at the witness's version from
R0) is decided by BMC and replayed: the real Rollup stores the hash of the header the model built, with zeros where the
Go encoders write the sequencer-set hash. What the build did not reach: SMT over the hand-written machines (the
eight M1 trace classes, the M2 site laws and the view records), which leaves P1 and P4 short; most BMC questions over
the full Rollup end unknown, at the preregistered rlimit or at the build's wall-clock and memory guards, so the
no_path predictions (P5, P7's known_limitation, P10's T1 and F-M2-42) are not reached; and the emitted tests cover
only the codec sides of P11.

## Done

- **Solver pin and encodings (E-SMT).** `holon/smt/run.py` runs only the pinned z3 (`config/holon.json` "z3": version,
  binary and library sha256, random_seed 0, rlimit 200,000,000 per query, one thread). `holon/smt/encode.py` writes
  ground QF_AUFBV with the "; holon-smt" header (terms and instances: cf, nz, vh_top, dense, time, cost, cf_asserted),
  E1 hash terms (fresh word + preimage, ground injectivity and functionality over content digests), NZ, E3 (dense blob
  vector), E5 (time chain), E6 (checked arithmetic) and E7 (cost terms). `instances()` gives the header's counts.
  TestEngines passes except the P3 bookkeeping check before the fuzz fix (it passes now).
- **Layout queries (E-LAY).** `gen/M3/refute/smt/layouts/{enc_sol,load_sol}.smt2`: both unsat (exactly one path of the
  extracted document applies to every input).
- **Codec engine (Q2).** `holon/smt/codec.py` (linked component, `gen/M3/codec/header_length.json`, the semantics of
  `holon.gen.linked`) and `holon/smt/facts01.py` (facts 0.1, `facts/morph_header.json`, the semantics of
  `holon.gen.model`). Every one of the 88 codec laws agrees with its status: sat with an accepted candidate on exactly
  the 7 REFUTED laws, unsat on the 78 PROVED and the 3 PAR-weak UNKNOWN laws. Each sat candidate's counter-law
  (`holon.gen.linked` counter for M3, `holon.gen.laws` + `holon.gen.proofs.counter` for M0) is accepted by bend2
  (ALL PROOFS CHECK) and by BendTT.
- **Re-find (Q1).** The codec and domain classes C01-C03 and C23-C26 are re-found (sat, candidate accepted by both
  kernels, signature matching). The 19 trace classes C04-C22 are not run (no SMT over the hand machines; Not done).
- **Fuzz (Q7).** `holon/fuzz.py`: boundary values first (the literals of the named functions' domain conditions), then
  the seeded stream (seed 20261014, one stream per law). On the three PAR-weak laws: 4096 candidates each, none
  separates the sides.
- **BMC (E-BMC).** `holon/bmc.py`: a symbolic evaluator over the holon-facts/0.3 term language of the extracted Rollup
  (storage families as words or arrays, loops unrolled with overflow havoc, R0 decoded from the deployment's init
  capture, raw slots and known preimages), cone-of-influence slicing with widening levels (an under-approximate level's
  sat is a candidate; only the full cone's unsat decides k), the answer model of refute.json "bmc" in
  `spec/M3/refute_env.json`, iterative deepening k = 1..8. Every path is replayed on the real Rollup through the
  binding's step harness; a path the real contract does not reproduce is recorded unknown ("spurious candidate"), never
  path.
- **Rule K3 by BMC (new in this build).** For each commit_class record the question `cc:v<version>`: a successful
  commitBatch at the witness's version from R0, observing the Solidity encoder's sink (the keccak256 of the header it
  builds, of that version's length). The path's inputs are pinned and every hash word is set to the real digest of the
  content the model gives it (hash concretization), so the model's header is the real one. The emitted Foundry test
  runs the commit and asserts that the contract stored the keccak256 of a model header whose bytes at every cell where
  the two encoders differ are the Solidity side's (`vm.record` / `vm.accesses`, no op table).
- **Emitted tests.** `holon/gen/foundry.py` (a witness's calls from calldata, `vm.prank`, per-step outcome asserted,
  storage words or stored hashes asserted) and `holon/gen/gotest.py` (the extracted Go method called on the witness's
  record, built by reflection from field paths; asserts the bytes the model gives the Go side). `gen/M3/tests/` with its
  manifest; `holon refute --milestone M3 --only emit` rewrites it byte for byte.
- **Final filter.** `holon.filter.final_bin` (F1-F4, `holon-filter/1.0`) agrees with `tests_m3/_i40.py::
  expected_final_bin` on every input of TestFilterRule; `bin_of` is unchanged. G6 recomputes REFUTED bins with the
  final rule when a record carries a concretization, and G1's regeneration overlays the committed concretization and
  final bin. The v2 schema gains `known_limitation` and `concretization`.
- **The fixture (P9).** T401: the realize law is UNKNOWN under the current refuter and REFUTED by BMC with the 7-step
  chain arm, s1..s5, fire (counter-law accepted by bend2 and BendTT; the emitted test and holon's replay pass). T402
  (the guarded variant): no_path, largest_k 8.
