## B6: residue_bound

**The idea.** A conjunct is about the word at one key after two steps. residue_bound proves, for each key and each step,
a *fact*: the word at the key after the step, related to the step's pre-state. A fact is one of

- **kept**: every store of the step's program is at a key that differs from it (I68's st_frame);
- **transported**: a store at the key itself (st_same), kept by the later stores; the fact then names the stored word;
- **bounded by a split store**: a store at a key that meets it in one word pair (x, y), whose value is
  `w_add(sload(st, key), D)`. By cases on `w_eq(x, y)`, the word grows by at most N(D) (the wrap bound
  `N(w_add(a, b)) <= N(a) + N(b)`), or the store is a frame;
- **bounded by an accumulating loop**: below.

The class finds each fact by a dry run of the step's chain (`Plan.probe`). The budget D of a split store is read off the
store, and a budget read at the second step's pre-state is rewritten through the first step's transports of the words
it reads.

**The conjuncts of C_o12_m3:**
- (a) is kept by setPause and bounded by challengeState's accumulating loop, whose budget is proofWindow read in s;
- (b) is kept by both steps;
- (c) is bounded by setPause's split store at `batchChallengeReward[challenger]`. Its budget, the deposit
  `challenges[batchChallenged].challengeDeposit` read after x, is x's `msg.value`: batchChallenged and the deposit are
  both transported through challengeState.

**The accumulating loop** (challengeState's loop at Rollup.sol:489-493). Each iteration adds D to the word at
`batchDataStore[c0 + it].finalizeTimestamp`, in the overlay and in a shadow store the loop reads, and `it` steps by one
under the checked increment `c0 + it + 1`. The invariant, over the record's own pre-state s, overlay ov, shadow c1 and
index it, is

- `N(c0 + it) == N(c0) + N(it)`;
- ov and c1 agree at the fact's key k;
- c1 holds at k either the pre-state's word, or a word at most N(pre-state's) + N(D) with `N(k) < N(c0 + it)` (k
  already met);
- or the record's code is not 0 (a revert ends the loop).

It is the last component of the loop's observation; the others are the record's fixed fields. Each iteration keeps it
(the iteration's tree from the mirror; at the step, a hit when `c0 + it == k` and a frame otherwise). The fuel induction
keeps it over the whole loop. The key-step lemmas (`ks_0`, `ks_i`, `ks_j`, `ks_s`) show that the key starts at N(c0)
and steps by exactly one. No word comparison is needed: the order is on Nat.

**The library** (holon.sorts.residue_lemmas, every lemma proved in the file):
- the Nat toolkit;
- `w_adc_nat`: Word.adc read as Nat;
- the model's u_add, u_addc and carry tests per limb;
- `w_bridge`: `N(w_add(a, b)) + carry 2^256 == N(a) + N(b)`;
- `w_wrap`;
- `wl_bound`: `N(w) + 1 + wcn(w) == 2^256`;
- `nw` and `okn`: no carry below 2^256, or under w_add_ok;
- the key-step lemmas;
- an assoc/comm normalizer for the Nat rearrangements.

The store and order lemmas are holon.disch's.

**What the contract's text of B6 describes and residue_bound does not need.** B6 describes the loop's exact effect,
each passed check's equation carried down the chain (the convoy), w_le as Nat order, and the bit-field lemmas. On
C_o12_m3 the bound invariant replaces the exact effect, and no conjunct reads a packed field or needs a check's
equation, so none of these is built.

## Surprises

1. **bend2's stack, near its limit at a loop statement.** A statement lemma for the loop statement had the goal
   `s_challengeState_2(..)`. Its caller's conversion into it unfolded the model's loop on both sides and compared the two
   fuels' unary numerals, `U32.to_nat(4097)`, 4097 levels deep. The contract law's proof then crashed bend2 now and then
   with "the machine stack overflowed" (the nondeterministic crash of Q34) under the default 8 MB stack. `612b276` steps a
   loop statement in its caller's lemma and writes the proof's fuel as the literal `4097n`; the proof now checks with a
   3 MB stack. (CI's launcher gives the checker a 1 GB stack, so CI would not have shown it.)
2. **A closed power of two must never meet a conversion.** The checker compares two different terms by unfolding both;
   a comparison that reaches `2^32` or `2^256` written as a numeral expands it and overflows. residue_bound's library
   passes every power of two as an opaque parameter and connects terms in steps whose types do not contain one.
3. **B6's text describes more than the contract needs.** It names the loop's exact effect, the convoy, `w_le` as Nat
   order and the bit-field lemmas. C_o12_m3 needs none of them: a bound invariant replaces the exact effect, and no
   conjunct reads a packed field or needs a check's equation. They are what C_o12_m3_open needs (P8).
4. **The binding pinned the emitter's first output.** `801670d` bound the law before B6 changed its statement helpers;
