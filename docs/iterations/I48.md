## Summary

Iteration I48 constructs the historical defect evaluation corpus, the unsolved control-obligations suite, and the deterministic isomorphic renaming engine required for the empirical evaluation and limitations sections of the paper (`§12 Limitations`, `§13 Evaluation`):

1. **Historical defect corpus (`spec/eval/corpus_manifest.json`, `spec/eval/excerpts/CORPUS-01..20/`)**: 20 real historical bug-fix commits across 4 production rollup/blockchain repositories (`morph`, `arbitrum-nitro`, `optimism`, `taiko`; 5 entries per system; 15 Solidity and 5 Go), double-labelled (`label_1`, `label_2`) across all 8 universal rules (`PAR`, `SORT`, `NAT`, `OWN`, `DISCH`, `FACT`, `UNDO`, `SYM`) with Cohen's $\kappa = 1.0$. Every entry records its exact `pre_fix_commit`, `fix_commit` (`fix_commit^1 == pre_fix_commit`), `pre_fix_blob_sha` (git blob SHA-1), `fix_blob_sha` (git blob SHA-1), `patch_sha256` (SHA-256 of `patch.diff`), and self-contained `pre_fix.<ext>`, `fix.<ext>`, and `patch.diff` excerpts.
2. **Unsolved control obligations (`spec/eval/controls/`, `gen/M4/controls/`, `results/M4/controls/`)**: 12 control obligations (`CTRL-01`..`CTRL-12`, laws `L_ctrl_01`..`L_ctrl_12`) over `eval_ctrl_machine.facts.json` (`holon-facts/0.2`, `holon-ir/0.3`):
   - **6 `FALSE_BEYOND_BOUND` controls (`CTRL-01`..`CTRL-06`)**: false laws whose minimal counterexample lies strictly beyond the evaluation search budget (`prefixDepth=2`, `maxPrefixes=100`, `natMax=5`). Unassisted HOLON abstains with `status == "UNKNOWN"` (`false_positive_proofs == 0`) and records the exact search bound; each control's out-of-budget counterexample witness is independently compiled into `gen/M4/controls/ground_truth/<law_id>/{LAWS,PROOF}.bend` and accepted by `bend check` (`COUNTER_LAW_ACCEPTED`).
   - **6 `TRUE_UNPROVED` controls (`CTRL-07`..`CTRL-12`)**: true laws requiring inductive lemmas or state-field case splits outside HOLON's unassisted tactic library (`refl`, `case_split_guard`, `nat_ind`, `list_ind`, `eq_chain`, `evaluate`). Unassisted HOLON abstains with `status == "UNKNOWN"` (`false_negative_refutations == 0`) and records all 6 tried tactic generators; each control's handcrafted inductive/case-analysis Bend 2 proof is independently accepted by `bend check` (`PROOF_ACCEPTED`).
3. **Deterministic isomorphic renaming (`holon/eval/rename.py`)**: seed-parameterized bijective identifier renaming (`rename`, `unrename`, `rename_facts`, `unrename_facts`, `rename_law`, `unrename_law`, `validate_mapping`) for Solidity, Go, `holon-facts/0.2` JSON, and `holon-ir/0.3` law JSON. Scrubs comments and string literals, preserves language/IR reserved tokens (including the mandatory `log` state field and structural expression tags), guarantees collision-free forward/reverse bijections, and round-trips all 20 corpus excerpts (`pre_fix` and `fix`), `eval_ctrl_machine.facts.json`, and `L_ctrl_01..12` byte-for-byte.
4. **Deterministic evaluation report (`results/eval/I48_corpus_controls.json`)**: summarizes corpus verification, control calibration (`total_controls=12`, `unknown_count=12`, `false_positive_proofs=0`, `false_negative_refutations=0`, `confidently_wrong=0`, `missing_bound_count=0`, `calibration_ok=True`), and the structured `limitations_table` for `§12 Limitations` and `§13 Evaluation`.
