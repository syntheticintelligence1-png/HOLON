## Surprises

- **No counted prediction missed.** All 13 came out on the first run (see the correlations above). This is reported as an outcome, not as a confirmation of 13 independent claims.
- **`"#"` (the length of a sequence) overlaps every term.** The contract's overlap rule calls any term that is neither `"*"` nor numeric symbolic, so the length read `#` overlaps an append at `len(win)` and a truncation from `idx-lfi..`. That gives E2 from each commit and from `revertBatch` to the observer `lci_of` (`finalizeBatch`'s whole write `*` overlaps it under any reading). It changes no prediction, but it adds E2 to laws that name `lci_of` (the three `L_disch_delay_*` laws with a Trace binder, `L_undo_BVH`), all of which have E2 through other atoms too. See Questions.
- **E2 is everywhere in the rollup** (174 atoms), mostly through scalar registers many actions read (`inch`, `rri`, `paused`, `t`) and the precise `win`/`chs` index terms. So every M1 law with any computed level has E2 as its strongest, and E6 (11 TRANS pairs sharing `batch`, `commit` or `state`) is never the maximum there. This is why I14-P13's "M1 has none" holds.
- **E2 runs through the ghost register `s_tag`** (8 atoms: the three commits write it at `lci+1` and `revertBatch` writes it whole; `commitState` and `commitBatchWithProof` read it at `parent+1`). The contract does not exclude ghost carriers from E2; I13 does not read ghosts for sorts. See Questions.
- **E5 found eight atoms beyond P7**, all Time against Time: `ef + D < t` in the three commits and `w(.., lci0).bdo + D < t` in `commitBatchWithProof`, `bdf > t` in `challengeState` and `finalizeBatch`, `ch.s + W <= t` in `proveState`, and the observer `kappa`. P7 did not claim exclusivity.
- **The `chs` carrier is unresolved in 12 pairs**: `revertBatch` both clears (`clear_chs`) and scans (`any_open`) the challenge list through kindless builtins. This was not predicted. It does not move any law's levels, since every law naming `revertBatch` has E2 through other carriers.
- **The honesty suite runs in under 2 s**; the pass takes about 0.15 s.

## Questions for the human

1. **Length reads.** Should `"#"` (a read of the length only) overlap a write at a symbolic index such as `len(win)`, as the contract's literal "any other term is symbolic" says, or only writes that can change the length (appends, truncations, whole writes)? The answer moves E2 atoms into and out of `lci_of`, but no law's computed levels (each law naming `lci_of` has E2 through other atoms).
2. **Ghost carriers as E2 evidence.** `s_tag` is ghost state, written and read only by the model's ghost bookkeeping. E2 through it is evidence that the model relates two paths, not that the code does. Should E2 skip ghost carriers (`ghost_state`, ghost record fields), as I13 does for sorts? No law's levels change today: the laws naming the commits or `revertBatch` have E2 through real registers.
3. **Unresolved pairs and laws.** L1–L4 never use unresolved entries, so `L_nat_eta2` and `L_tag_BVH` have no computed level at all, and their records say `finding` by default. Their real write-then-read goes through `win_last`, `set_last_csr`, `zero_csr` and `tags_exact`. Declaring access kinds for those builtins in the target library (a new library version, through a prereg) would turn the pairs into E2 atoms. Is that the intended route, or should GEVID report unresolved pairs touching a law's named edges?
