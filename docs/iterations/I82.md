# I82 (= I46a): the blind pack, the comparison generator and the agreement metric. 6 of 7 counted predictions held; P4 missed in part

I82 is I46's tooling half. It exists so that I46's blind rerun and its generated comparison can run as soon as the last
M3 iteration lands. I46 keeps its number for the blind rerun and the M3 exit.

The key is `iterations.I82` in `spec/M3/predictions.json`, and the contract is the docstring of `tests_m3/test_blind.py`:
- K1-K9 for the pack;
- L1-L6 for the leak scan;
- A1-A9 and G1 for the map and the comparison;
- B1-B6 for the metric;
- R for the commits.

The instrument is `tests_m3/_i82.py`, which imports nothing from holon/. Two sidecars are fixed at the prereg:
- `spec/M3/lib/blind_pack.json`, I46's protocol;
- `spec/M3/lib/appendixA.json`, the record sets, the back-test tables, and the curated links with their citations.

BUILD_PLAN row I82 is `d037c410`. The prereg (C_K) is `4cf64460`, on the lead `00b12f70` (after I28's build merge).
The lead moved three times during the build. I80 and I81 were merged in as `336d5f03` (lead `3da104c1`), and I72's
build as `5ae5f36f` (lead `92c98e40`).

What was built:
- **`tools/blind_pack.py`.** It writes I46's sealed bundle as an uncompressed pax tar from git objects only, with:
  - the pinned morph tree under `target/`;
  - the tooling roots under `tooling/`;
  - the whitepaper less §2, §11, Appendix A, Appendix B and the seven "Morph instances" lists.

  It also writes `manifest.json` (every member's sha256, the exclusions by rule, the redactions) and `leaks.json`. The
  leak list is computed and reported, never filtered. One commit packs to the same bytes, in about 9 s.
- **`holon report --milestone M3 [--check] [--results SET=DIR]`** (`holon/report.py`). It writes
  `spec/M3/appendixA_map.json` and `docs/M3_COMPARISON.md`. It reads records only, takes about 0.2 s, and names no law
  id. G1 now calls `holon.report.regenerate_check` with its regenerated records in place of the committed ones.
- **`holon compare --blind DIR [--lead DIR] [--milestone ..] [--out F] [--threshold X]`** (`holon/blind.py`). It gives
  `blind_agreement` over the statuses of α-equivalent statements, with the matched-statement fraction.
  `holon compare --snapshot SRC --into DST` projects a scratch tree.

matches, not adjudicated leaks; the full list is `audit/blind/pack/leaks.json`.

**The real leaks, by place:**

- **The redacted whitepaper: 34 matches.** I46's protocol leaves several passages that carry the case study:

  - Appendix C, the version-1 header layout, with the witnesses 249 and 257, and D1 and O2 (lines 1106-1117);
  - Appendix F's sketches (lines 1159-1160);

  - §15.1, which names L_par_hdr_v0, L_par_hdr_v1, L_par_hdr_v2 and L_dom_v0;
  - §15.2's M1 and M3 rows;
  - the abstract's "prior manual analysis" and "four obstructions the manual analysis missed".

  - `holon/consumers.py` (14): the Go consumer replays of `L_tag_BVH` and `L_fact_Z`, and the 257-byte header, 5
    witness matches;

   Your note asked for committed records only. If you want that reading, it is a one-line change to the call, but it
