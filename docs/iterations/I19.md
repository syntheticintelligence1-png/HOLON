### Storage families (`holon/frontends/solidity/storage.py`)

Storage comes from the compiler: a sidecar with `"transitions"` makes `compile.py` select `{"*": {"*": ["storageLayout"], "": ["ast"]}}` (a layout sidecar keeps I17's AST-only selection, so `morph_header.extracted.json` is byte-identical). A family is a state variable the contract declares itself, in declaration order, with the compiler's `slot` and `offset`, `kind` scalar / mapping / array, the mapping `key`, the `value` type label, `width` = 8 × `numberOfBytes` for an elementary value, and `fields` (name, type, width) for a struct value. Constants and immutables are not storage. The contract's own constants are folded from the AST (I17 W4, plus `keccak256` of a literal) and written as `0x`-hex (bytesN, address, literals) or decimal strings.

Rollup: 24 families at slots 151–174, names, kinds, types and widths as P1 lists; `constants` = `{EIP1967_PROXY_ADMIN_SLOT, ZERO_VERSIONED_HASH}`.

### Transitions (`holon/frontends/solidity/transitions.py`)

One structured symbolic walk per external or public function the contract declares (29 for Rollup, `receive` included under its kind's name):
- modifiers inlined in order (`_` walks the next modifier, then the body), internal, private, public and library-internal calls inlined with virtual dispatch over the contract's linearization, storage pointers bound by reference;
- both branches of every `if` walked, then before else, under the branch condition; locals, storage and memory merged after the `if` into `["?", c, a, b]`; a branch that always reverts contributes nothing to the merge; a branch that may leave by `return`, `break` or `continue` puts the condition under which control goes on into the context of what follows (early returns of inlined functions are merged into the caller's value and state);
- storage as a functional store: a read is `["storage", f]` (with `["map", …]`, `["member", …]`, `["index", …]` paths) before any write, the written value after a write of the same path, `["store", base, path, v]` when a write may alias;
- memory as I17's regions (W5), with writes kept per region: `mload` resolves to the stored word or, over a `calldatacopy`, to `["word", input, k]`; `keccak256` over a region that is exactly an input's copy is `["keccak256", input]`;
- loops: a dry run that records nothing finds what the body writes; locals it changes become `["phi", loop, name]` (pointers keep their region), written families `["phi", loop, ["storage", f]]`, written regions get a `["loop", span]` entry; then the body is walked once, recording;
- Yul: I17's subset plus `sload`, `blobhash`, `YulIf`, `revert`/`invalid` and a few environment builtins; `YulForLoop`, `YulSwitch`, Yul functions and other builtins are debt (`unsupported`), what they assign is havocked to `["unknown", span, name]`.

Per action: `selector`, `src`, `modifiers`, `principal`, `guards` (first reach, one per span; `expr` carries the branch context: `["||", ["!", K], c]` for a require under K, `["!", K]` for a revert), `effects` (sorted by (src, family) as strings), `cost.terms`, `edges`. The document is canonical (184 797 bytes) and byte-identical across runs; `assumptions` names A-env, A-implicit, A-loop, A-reentrancy and A-span.

### What changed in the document format

All additions are new keys. No I19 key changed its meaning. The keys are documented in the module docstring of `holon/frontends/solidity/transitions.py` and closed in `spec/schemas/facts_0_3.schema.json` (`tr_reach`, `tr_check`, `tr_panic`, `tr_call`, `tr_write`, `tr_mstore`, `tr_event`, `tr_loop`, `tr_param`).

- **Every record** carries `seq`, an integer unique in the action, in walk order. It also carries `src` and `stack`: the spans of the inlined calls and of the modifier invocations entered, outermost first. A record made inside a loop's generic iteration also carries `loop`, the seq of that loop.
- **Per action:**
  - `declared_in`, `params`, `returns`, `payable`, `mutability`;
  - `guards[].reaches`: the first reach's expr is the entry's expr, and an assert's reach has `code: 1`;
  - `checks`, `panics`, `calls`, `writes`, `mstores`, `events`, `loops`, `post`;
  - `order`: every check in walk order. The transaction reverts at the first check whose expr is false.
- **Document level:** `inherited_storage`, with `declared_in` read from the AST. Struct fields gain the compiler's `slot` and `offset`. Nested mappings gain `keys`, and fixed arrays gain `length`.
- **New terms:**
  - `fits`/`sfits` (the exact range of a checked operation), `callok`/`caught`, `new`/`alloc`;
  - `carried`/`iter`/`count`/`exit`/`returned`/`sum` (loop symbols), `when` and `mloop` (memory entries);
  - `trunc`/`sext`, the signed operators, `addmod`/`mulmod`/`wbyte`/`signextend`, `push`/`pop`, `input`.
  - `phi` and the Yul `not` are gone.
- **Simplification.** Terms are simplified as they are built (nested conditionals, boolean collapses, substitution of equality conditions, `x*1`, `(x+c)+d` and others). They are also simplified when rendered: inside a conditional its condition is known, and inside `a || b` the operand `!a` is known. A rendered term of more than 400 000 nodes is an extraction error (exit 3).
- **Assumptions.**
  - A-span is replaced by A-reach: every reach is recorded.
  - A-implicit is narrowed to two exemptions: the ABI decoding of dynamic calldata, and the range of array elements of narrow types (wording corrected in round 2, V19-06: this line named only the first, while the committed assumption text names both).
  - A-loop now describes the transformer.
  - A-env covers `callok`.
- **Coverage.** EmitStatement is extracted (tag `event`). Yul `for`/`switch`/functions and `try` are no longer debt.

The loop transformer works in two steps:
1. It finds the carried set (locals, Yul variables, storage families, memory regions) by a fixpoint of dry runs, each with the names found so far bound to fresh `["carried", loop, name]` symbols.
2. A name to which every continuing path adds a loop-invariant step gets a closed form `entry + d*["iter", loop]`.

A loop without break or return whose head compares such a name with a loop-invariant bound is counted by I19's header rule. Otherwise `count` is `["count", loop]`, defined by the record. After the loop:
- a closed-form name holds its closed form at iter = count;
- a name of a loop that ends only by break holds its break value at iter = count, when that value has a closed form;
- every other name holds `["exit", loop, name]`;
- a memory region whose per-iteration writes have closed forms gets one `["mloop", loop, ["iter", loop], count, [writes]]` entry;
- return paths inside a loop return under `["returned", loop, j]`.

#### Booleans (V19-02)

The verifier proposed treating a bool read from storage as a boolean (0 or 1). With the pinned compiler that is unsound. A forge probe on solc 0.8.24, optimizer off, cancun (`tests_m2/fixtures/i19_followup/round2/semantics/test/Semantics.t.sol`, run by `TestCompilerSemantics`) shows the following for a bool slot holding byte 2:
- the read is 2: the compiler loads the raw byte and does not clean it;
- `flag == true` is true: the compiler cleans both sides of `==` and `!=`;
- the getter returns 1, an indexed `bool` topic and the log data are 1, `abi.encode` hashes 1, a copy to another storage bool stores 1, and a memory store keeps 1: the compiler cleans when it encodes or stores;
- `flag && flag2` with bytes 2 and 3 gives 3: `&&` keeps the raw value.

So the round-2 rules are:
- **Conditions are rendered by truthiness.** A term that stands where only its truthiness counts is rendered 1 or 0 when the enclosing conditionals already decide it. This covers a guard's, check's, panic's or call's `expr`, every `cond`, a loop's `head` and `break`, a principal's predicate, the operands of `!`, `&&` and `||`, and the condition of a conditional or a `when`. So `["?", ["!", s], 1, s]` renders 1 as a condition. As a value it stays, because s may be 2.
- **Value-exact rules in `ite`.** For c = x or c = !x: x is 0 where x fails (any word), and 1 where it holds when x is a boolean. When the branches agree at that value, the conditional is the other branch. So `ite(!x, 0, x)` and `ite(x, x, 0)` are x for any x, and `ite(!x, 1, x)` is 1 only for a boolean x.
- **Boolean leaves** are bool entry parameters, bool members of struct parameters (both checked by the ABI decoder before use) and bool results of external calls (checked inside `callok`). A storage read is never one.
- **Cleanup as the compiler does it.** `["!=", v, 0]` (or v itself when v is a boolean) is applied where the compiler cleans:
  - the action's `returns`, event `args` and `topics`, revert-data `args`, external call arguments and `abi.encode*`;
  - storage and memory stores of a bool (writes, struct constructors, inline arrays, pushes);
  - both operands of `==` and `!=` on bools.
- **`post`** leaves out a family whose simplified value is `["storage", f]`.

Morph effect:
- the four reaches of `Initializable.sol:133` are 1;
- `initialize.post` no longer lists `_initializing`;
- `setPause.post._paused` is `["param", "_status"]` (was `ite(_status, 1, 0)`);
- one branch of `setPause.post.inChallenge` is 0 (was `ite(inChallenge, 0, inChallenge)`);
- `paused.returns` and `batchChallengedSuccess.returns` are cleaned bools.

No other term changes. The probe also confirms the other round-2 rules:
- the topic hashing (V19-04);
- `new uint256[](2^64)` and `new bytes(2^64)` give Panic 0x41, while `2^64 - 1` fails with empty revert data (the probe sees out of gas, `InvalidOperandOOG`) (V19-05);
- a storage `push` has no length check, at any length up to 2^70;
- calldata shorter than the head reverts with empty data, and longer calldata is accepted;
- custom error data is the selector followed by `abi.encode(args)` (V19-03).

#### What changed in the document format (round 2; additive, documented in the module docstring and closed in `spec/schemas/facts_0_3.schema.json`)

- `guards[].error` (`tr_error`: `{name, signature, selector}`) and `guards[].args`; `guards[].reaches[].args`. The revert data is the selector followed by `abi.encode(args...)`. A custom error's name is `<Contract>.<Error>`. Its selector is checked against the compiler's `errorSelector`, and the extraction stops on a mismatch. A string reason (constant or not) gives `Error(string)`. A guard without `error` reverts with empty data, or with Panic(0x01) for an assert (`code` 1).
- `events[].topics`, required: the topic of each indexed argument in order:
  - a `string` or `bytes` argument gives `["keccak256", x]`, folded to an integer for a constant;
  - an array or struct gives `["keccak256", ["abi.inplace", x]]`;
  - any other argument gives its ABI word.

  `args` are ABI words.
- `checks[].kind` `calldatasize`: `[">=", ["env", "msg.data.length"], 4 + h]`, where h is the head size of the parameters' encoding. It comes after `callvalue` and before the `abi` range checks, as in solc's dispatcher. A fallback gets none: its `bytes` parameter is the raw calldata, which nothing decodes.
- `panics[].code` 65: `["<=", n, 2^64 - 1]` for `new T[](n)`, `new bytes(n)` and `new string(n)`.
- `returns` are ABI words. Conditions are rendered by truthiness, and the `ite` rules and the `post` rule are as above.
- A new term tag, `abi.inplace`.
- The A-implicit text now also names the Panic 0x41 of allocating a decoded dynamic value (part of the dynamic-calldata exemption) and out of gas.
