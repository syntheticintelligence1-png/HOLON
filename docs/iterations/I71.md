# I71 (I33's catalog key): UNDO's third sub-form, cancel. P1 and P2 held; P3-P6 held after a post-outcome search fix

I71 adds UNDO's third sub-form, `cancel`. It closes I27's back-test gap G5 (M1's `L_undo_cancel` had no M3 analogue)
and gives I33's O12 intent contract on M3 its scope law.
- **The rules.** U3', the cancel restore, and U4', the criterion `cond`, are computed apart from I27's U3 and U4. So
  I27's pairs, rejected pairs, residue bases and laws do not move.
- **The analysis.** The extracted Rollup has exactly one cancellation, (challengeState, setPause):
  - challengeState writes `inChallenge = true` (`writes[3]`) after pinning it to false (`guards[3]`);
  - setPause writes it back to false (`writes[3]`) under `_status && inChallenge`;
  - the residues are batchChallenged, batchDataStore and challenges, all statically live.
- **The law.** `L_undo_cancel_Rollup_challengeState_setPause`: after challengeState then setPause(true), every trace
  observes what it observes after setPause(true) alone. This is M1's reading of `L_undo_cancel`, with the premise that y
  also succeeds alone (Question H1).
- **The catalog.** holon-rules/1.7 is I28's 1.6, the same producer objects, plus UNDO's `cancel` producer.
  `holon.rules.undo.PRODUCERS` stays I27's {pair, residue}.

**The cancel law is REFUTED by the refund, but only after a search fix that the first outcome prompted.**
1. **First outcome.** The runner decided the law first: CI run 41013744445, job cancel, on `06d873f` (the tree
   `746d491`). It was UNKNOWN: "the candidate budget (200000) ran out after 112 prefix states; no witness" (1789 s).
2. **Cause.** Found with probes in a scratch worktree. No continuation step ever moved its caller: each kept its
   synthesized caller, and `UndoSearch.variants` varied only arguments and the clock.
   - setPause(true) credits challengeState's caller its deposit (`batchChallengeReward[challenger] += deposit`).
   - Only that caller's claimReward reads the credit.
   - So every depth-1 claimReward candidate ran as another account and reverted the same way in both runs
     ("invalid batchChallengeReward"). Depth 2 then spent the budget.
3. **Fix.** `6ef2a03`, made after that outcome: the continuation variants also try the caller of each step already in
   the trace, the latest first. It is generic: no action, family or law is named, and the pair and residue searches
   share it.
4. **Final outcome.** `813aa70`, holon run on this tree. REFUTED at prefix depth 1, after 35 prefix states and 295
   candidates:
   - prefix: commitBatch by 0x1002;
   - x: challengeState(1, its batch hash) by 0x1004, msg.value 1;
   - y: setPause(true) by the owner;
   - t: claimReward(0) by 0x1004.
   - **Kernels:** bend2 checks the counter-law (ALL PROOFS CHECK) and BendTT's verdict is `checked`.
   - **Replay:** on the binding's deployment it passes with calls_agree. claimReward pays the deposit after x;y and
     reverts after y alone.
   - **Filter:** K4's emitted Foundry tests pass and observe the obstruction. The final bin is `finding`.
5. **Counting.** P3-P6 hold on the final outcome. They are listed below as held after a post-outcome search fix, not as
   plainly held: whether they count is Question H5.
