## Surprises

- **O29's witness is a third party replaying a whitelisted sender's message for free.** The fee is computed for
  `_from`.
- **The value variant of a row step is replayed as a raw call of its calldata.** I69's rows carry no value, so the
  replayed commit carries no blob hashes. The legacy branch takes the zero versioned hash.
- **DB1 needed a fourth construct:** a struct value's one-member update in `replayMessage`.
- **The bundle laws enter the law sets of six earlier tests** (narrowed as above), and the evidence set needed the two
  documents.
- **:398#1 needs three steps, not the prediction's two.**
  - commitBatchWithProof's "l1msg delay" guard also needs `!rollupDelay`: the last committed batch must be newer than
    the message.
  - So the shortest path puts a successful commit between the enqueue and the guard, and at k = 3 I40's rlimit runs out
    at every cone level.
- **:263#1 and :393-394#0 are paths only near block.timestamp 2^256,** as I40 found. The queue's view gives the current
  timestamp, so `enqueueTime + rollupDelayPeriod` overflows only there.
- **:281#1's search did not finish.** Its first cone level at k = 2 ran past 88 minutes twice, and a container restart
  stopped it each time.
- **I40's final filter bins the five REFUTED bundle records pending, not finding.**
  - The filter reads I4 sides from `replay/M0-M2/report.json` and I67's Go-op report, not from
    `replay/M3/bundle_laws/report.json`.
  - So F3 cannot hold, although every replay passed.
