# I68 (the Q21 fix): structural storage keys in the M3 model. P1-P7 held; R3 and R5-R8 held; R1, R2 and R4 missed as written

I68 keys the M3 model's mapping storage structurally, as (base slot, offset, key path), instead of looking each slot up
in the law's keccak256 table parameter (Q21, I30's Question 1). Under an adversarial table the old store aliased every
mapping slot with slot 0. With structural keys the table is off the storage path: real slots are computed with the real
keccak256 only against real traces (R5), in the replays and in the deployment's conversion, and the idealisation is
named `A-keccak-structural` in spec/M3/ledger.json. No statement changes; the typed laws are rebound through remodel
lines.

The key is `iterations.I68` in `spec/M3/predictions.json`. The contract is the docstring of
`tests_m3/test_structural_keys.py` (S1-S13). The prereg is `54bb76f` (C_K, on the BUILD_PLAN row `4db1669` over the base
`2ef07b6`), with its self-check fold `fe0c6bb`.

**Result:** the 14 DISCH laws the store opens are PROVED, each with a checked, reachable inhabitant, and DISCH stands at
27 PROVED, 7 REFUTED and 1 UNKNOWN (13, 7 and 15 at the prereg). M3's PROVED count goes from 87 to 101 and its unknown
rate from 0.184 to 0.081. Every other typed law keeps its status. The toy's kept-register law is PROVED by a Reach
induction (tag_exact). R5 agrees on I20's corpus, the keys check finds no collision, and every remade proof and
counter-law checks in bend2 and BendTT within budget. Three regression checks miss on their own wording, not on a
statement, a status or a helper:
- R1's path check counts the 14 inhabitant law files that the flips need.
- R2 claims a passed replay for a FACT law whose replay had already failed at the prereg.
- R4 reads the DISCH, UNDO and FACT sections as if each carried the prelude's helpers. They import them from the
  typed model, as they did at the prereg.
