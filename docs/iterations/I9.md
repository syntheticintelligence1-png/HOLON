## Surprises

1. **Two preregistered tests contradict I9 by construction.** I may not edit existing `tests_m2` files, so they fail and are reported (Questions 1 and 2):
   - `tests_m2/test_records_v2.py::TestRecordsV2::test_kernels_and_independence_are_honest` (I1) asserts `I1-shared` whenever BendTT checked. With a checked lean4 kernel the M0 records are `I2` (I9-P3), so its 8 M0 subtests fail. Its intent, that BendTT alone never earns I2, still holds and is now checked by `tests_m2/test_lean_records.py::TestHonesty`.
   - `tests_m2/test_target_agnostic.py` (I6) pins `GEN_PIN` over every file under `gen/M0` and `gen/M1`, and `RECORDS_PIN` over the M0/M1 records modulo `seconds` and `generator_sha256`. I9's outcome paths are exactly `gen/M0/lean/` and `results/M0/`, so `test_generated_files_unchanged` and `test_records_unchanged_modulo_seconds_and_generator_hash` fail. Nothing else moved: computed the tests' way over the I9 tree, the gen hash without `gen/M0/lean/` is exactly `GEN_PIN` (`6f37a5ff…`), and the records hash with the lean4 entry removed and `I2` read as `I1-shared` is exactly `RECORDS_PIN` (`38c704e6…`).
2. **Lean's code generator rejects the 257-field header constructor** ("constructor 'Model.Hdr.mk' has too many fields"). The witness definitions are `noncomputable`: they are only reduced by the elaborator and the kernel, never compiled.
3. **Plain `rfl` does not scale to the round-trip laws.** `dec_go (enc_sol h) = some (view h)` hit the elaborator's default heartbeat limit (200000) in `whnf`, because the decoder re-runs the encoder's write sequence under every getter slice. The per-cell lemmas (one per encoder output, by `rfl`) bring each file to 6 to 9 s. `rw` then closed the codec goals by itself, so the generator uses `rewrite` and an explicit `rfl`.
4. **The flipped-equality canary is rejected for a shallow reason.** The flipped claim is `≠`, and `rfl` fails with "Expected the goal to be a binary relation", not with a definitional mismatch. Any false claim must be rejected, so I9-P4 holds, but it says little about the kernel. G7 therefore also states `L_par_hdr_ssvh` (REFUTED) as a theorem through `pin_reduce`; Lean rejects it with "The left-hand side ... is not definitionally equal to the right-hand side". The same holds for `L_par_hdr_v2`.
5. **The two models differ in their carriers, on purpose.** Bend header cells are `U32` with `57005` for an undefined byte, so Bend's PROVED laws quantify over a superset of byte-valued headers. Lean cells are `BitVec 8` with definedness as `Option`, so Lean's theorems quantify over byte-valued headers only: the facts' domain (A-width, A-version). The counter-laws are over the same byte-valued witnesses in both. The differential test checks that the two agree on concrete inputs, including reverts and unwritten cells.
6. **Axioms.** The PROVED laws use `propext` (from `simp only [.., and_true]` on the premises). `L_dom_hdr`'s counter-law also uses `propext`; the two codec counter-laws use no axiom at all.
7. The `leanprover--lean4---v4.34.0` toolchain under `~/.elan/toolchains` is a link to `/root/lean-4.34.0-linux`. `holon/lean.py` runs that toolchain's `lake` and `lean` by absolute path and never goes through the `elan` proxy.
