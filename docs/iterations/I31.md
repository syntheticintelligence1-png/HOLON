### (2) The missing proof routes (review finding 1)

All new generator code is generic: it names no law, action, class or target. `TestGenericCode` passes, and so does
`tests_m2.test_target_agnostic_exhaustive`.

- **Word-order lemmas** (`holon/disch/order.py`, new). The library is generated once and emitted into every DISCH
  proof file, where it is checked with the proof. Nothing is assumed. bend2 accepts the library alone in 3.5 s and
  BendTT in 5.7 s. Bottom up:
  - `Word.cmp`'s flip and composition, by induction on the width. Each step is a generated case table over the head
    bits and the tails' comparisons (216 and 36 cases).
  - The same facts over U32.
  - `wcmp`, the lexicographic comparison of the 8 limbs, with `M.w_lt == Cmp.is_lt(wcmp)` proved level by level
    against the model's `u_lt2` nesting.
  - `w_trans` and `w_flip`.
  - The three facts the routes use: `w_lt_le` (a < b <= c), `w_le_lt` (a <= b < c) and `w_le_le` (a <= b <= c).
  - The transport store fact `st_same`, `sload(sstore(s, k, v), k) == v`, via `w_eq`'s reflexivity from
    `Word.cmp`'s.
- **Time monotonicity** (`TimeMono`).
  - Each clock atom of B is classified as rising or falling with the clock: `x < t`, `x <= t`, `t < x`, `t <= x`,
    `w_gt`/`w_ge` variants, and a signed difference `A - t` that does or does not realize. The last is the stale
    challenge's form, `is_some(realize(sub(A, T{t})))`; the `sub_*` definition is read from the typed model.
  - Each atom gets a one-step proof from the order lemmas. `tl_blk` combines them by cases on B's atoms.
  - The law's wrapper derives B at `time(e)` from the premise's B at `t` and `t <= time(e)`. Every route then states
    B at `time(e)`.
  - A clock atom of another form is refused, by name.
  - This is time monotonicity as the contract and the whitepaper state it: block.timestamp never decreases across
    steps, so a condition that rises with the clock stays true.
- **chain_revert with arithmetic.** A contradiction between B and a check is now decided under the implications
  between comparisons of one term with literals (for example `x < 2` gives `x < 3`).
  - Each implication is an instance of `w_lt_le` or `w_le_lt`, with the literals' order computed (`{==}`).
  - Implications are hypotheses of the generated case lemma, and the cases they exclude are closed by them.
- **Transport** (qualified after review: built, but **no accepted proof uses it**; only its library lemma `st_same`
  is checked in both kernels, and no planted fixture exercises the route itself). A store of a literal word at a slot
  B reads:
  - the slot invariants are carried per state, with one statement lemma per invariant state;
  - `st_same` gives the slot's word after the store;
  - at the end of the path, B of the stored words follows from B in the pre-state by cases on B's atoms, with Bend
    evaluating the closed atoms.
  - A store of a word that is not a literal at a slot B reads is refused, by name: "a bit-field update of a packed
    slot needs bit-field lemmas, not built". This is the case for every store at a slot a basis condition reads in
    this document: initialize3 and setPause write `_initialized`/`_initializing` and `_paused` by masked updates.
- **external_none** now also uses the frame and transport routes: B holds in the post-state and contradicts c.
  Before, only a reverting step was accepted.

Checked in both kernels before the run (proof files generated from the real section and model):
- the seven revert laws that have routes;
- rev_363's challengeState and initialize4 (time monotonicity);
- TI3's initialize2 (the arithmetic implication);
- blk_77's initialize3 (frame);
- noext_132's claimProveRemaining, initialize3 and renounceOwnership (external_none by frame).

### (3) S3: I30's neighbourhood search (review finding 4)

`LawSearch.neighbourhood` runs I30's S3 on I30's machinery: the S3 loop of `holon.sorts.search3.Refuter.refute`,
`Explorer.successors_b` for the inserted steps and `Refuter._tail` for the rest of the prefix.
- **Seeds** (an interpretation, stated after review). I30's `Refuter.refute` seeds S3 from the same law's other entry
  points' witnesses. A DISCH law's entry points share one last step, so DISCH reads "the other entry points'
  witnesses" as the S1+S2 candidates of every other DISCH law of the same model, in law id order, each with its last
  step dropped. A law outside the run is searched with S1+S2 to give its witness. S3-found witnesses (ext/noext 132)
  do not seed later laws. The neighbourhoods are shared and built lazily on the shared model, whose learned keys feed
  successor generation (Surprise 8), so a law's S3 can depend on the laws searched before it; the outcome run's law
  order is the sorted order of the 35 ids (`b2-I31/run.log`).
- **Neighbourhood.** At every position, the end first: the prefix itself, then one to
  `neighbourhood_insertion_length_max` (2) successful steps inserted, then the rest of the prefix run again, with the
  clock never decreasing.
- **Last step.** The law's last step is tried from every prefix where B holds.
- **Budget.** At most `neighbourhood_prefixes_per_entry_point_max` (20000) prefixes, the sidecar's budget exactly.
  Every entry point is tried at each prefix.
- A witness's neighbourhood is generated once per model, in a fixed order, and shared by every law that searches it.
- **Time box.** `holon run --deadline` is a wall-clock time box on S3. A law it leaves undecided is UNKNOWN by the time
  box, and its bound says so (never "budget exhausted").
- The narrowed S3 and the dead `candidates(s3)` path were removed.

## Surprises

1. **The merge cannot satisfy the append-only check as written.** Both lines appended to `laws/STATEMENT_DIFF.jsonl`,
   so whatever the resolution, a committed version of the file was no longer a prefix. The check then re-validated
   every committed line against the working tree, and old reextraction lines failed: 253 problems. The fix is
   `merged_lines` (Done (0)). The same holds for `spec/bindings/changes.jsonl`, which uses the same helper.
2. **A merge hid I27's catalog change from `registry_changes`.** The registry was compared with the first parent only
   (Done (0)).
3. **The IR 0.3 schema rejected every `Word` binder after I27's addition** (two oneOf alternatives matched). I27's own
   UNDO laws had the same defect.
4. **The rewritten analysis agrees with the old code everywhere.** The copied parts of closure.py were therefore
   faithful to the contract on this document. The reading choices are listed in Done (1).
5. **The new routes change no blocking law's status.** Every blocking and external_none law has at least one action
   blocked by a mapping-slot store (Q21's structural keys), a loop (the mirror), or a packed-slot update (bit-field
   lemmas). Part 1's review attributed the missing blocking proofs to the three routes. The routes were indeed
   missing, but they were not the only gap.
6. **The DISCH bindings depend on a run output.** Each DISCH law's binding cone reaches the DISCH section that
   `holon run` generates, so binding before the run gives a cone G2 later rejects. The DISCH laws are therefore bound
   after the outcome run.
7. **The run must not read the working tree.** `holon run` loads `holon/js/eval3.mjs` and lazily imported modules
   from the checkout while it runs, so editing code during a 7-hour run would change what it executes. The outcome run
   therefore ran in a `git archive` snapshot of the outcome code at `b5c98ab`, and only its outcome paths were copied
   back.
8. **The exploration depends on when it runs.** Explored lazily, interleaved with the first law's syntheses (the killed
   attempt), depth 2 held 756 distinct states. Explored completely up front (the outcome run), it held 771. The shared
   model's learned hash keys and answers evidently feed back into S2's successor generation. The outcome run explores
   first, so it is reproducible from its code, but the dependence is I30's machinery and is reported here, not fixed.
9. **Full budgets do not fit a session.** Without a time box, the uncapped S1+S2 of one law (blk_228: 16 entry points,
   B holding almost everywhere) ran for more than 2.5 hours. The 20000-prefix S3 neighbourhood per law adds about
   9 minutes per 500-15000 prefixes. The run therefore used a per-law time box (Done (4)). Every blocking UNKNOWN is
   "UNKNOWN by the time box", not by an exhausted budget.
10. **S3 earned its keep.** The two revert-request witnesses exist only through S3's inserted steps.
