## Done

### The definitions (`spec/M5/preregistration.json`, from the key)

The common rules:
- Every proportion carries its numerator, its denominator, a value and a Wilson 95% interval. The value is null when the denominator is 0.
- min_n is 5. A measure with a smaller denominator is labelled `insufficient_n`, and one with denominator 0 `not_measured`. Neither is ever judged `meets`.
- A verdict counts as decided only with checker evidence (`ALL PROOFS CHECK` and the law present).
- A member with zero records, or with more than one, is UNKNOWN.
- Constructed truth and documented truth are never pooled.
- Each metric has an overall verdict. It is `meets` only if every threshold meets.

**L6 (can versus will).**
- **Population.** F(M3): the 49 REFUTED records outside the bins model_defect, known_limitation and intended_bounded. They are frozen as `base.l6_findings`.
- **Payoffs.** A payoff is the sign vector of the class's holdings per asset: nonneg, negative or incomparable. No exchange rate is assumed, and gas is outside the value flows.
- **Verdicts.** The verdict is per class (rational, irrational, irrational_bounded or UNKNOWN). Only untrusted classes decide the finding's verdict.
- **Validity rules.** A record that fails any of V1–V8 is scored UNKNOWN:
  - V1: checker evidence;
  - V2: the expected classes;
  - V3: their trusted flags;
  - V4: horizon at least the witness length, and the witness length is the REFUTED witness's;
  - V5: the witness lies in the strategy space;
  - V6: the verdict follows from the classes;
  - V7: a rational class has a nonneg payoff and a strategy length within the horizon;
  - V8: the frozen value-flow document.
- **Measures.** The decided fraction (also without irrational_bounded), accuracy per truth source (decided and strict), the majority baseline, false_rational (the negative control) and false_irrational.
- **Thresholds.** Decided at least 0.5. Controls: decided accuracy at least 0.8, strict accuracy at least 0.75, and strict correct above the majority baseline. false_rational = 0 for every source and false_irrational = 0 for the controls. Corpus decided accuracy at least 0.8 with at least 5 items.

**L9 (environment).**
- **Classification.** Environment means exactly the L1 chain, DA and fetchers. `spec/M5/lib/environment_classes.json` classifies every assumption id that the M2 and M3 records hold. `A-env` is havoc over the L1 block and the blob-hash vector. The `env:` premise prefix is an axiom. Every other id is listed with the reason it is not environment for L9. An unlisted id is unclassified, which counts as an uncovered axiom.
- **Covered.** A law is covered only if every environment premise is a module premise naming a validated module.
- **Module families.** There are three: window, finality_depth and availability. The metric recomputes every prediction from the module's parameters, so a module record carries no predictions of its own.
- **Validation rules R1–R6:**
  - R1: the truth is non-circular;
  - R2: the observations are cut into K = 8 time slices, all held out unless slice 0 was fitted;
  - R3: at least 30 observations per slice;
  - R4: accuracy at least 0.95 on every held-out slice;
  - R5: at least 5 observations of each observed class;
  - R6: the parameters were pinned by the measuring key.
- **Measures.** Coverage (its denominator also holds base laws that lost their premises), base coverage over the 222 laws frozen in `base.l9_dependent`, new coverage, per-category counts, and sensitivity (only changes to a decided status, with evidence and a passed replay, are counted).
- **Thresholds.** Coverage at least 0.25, base coverage at least 0.1, a validated module in each of the three categories, and no unreplayed change.

**L10 (consistently wrong code).**
- **Denominator.** A mechanical census of the normative doc-comment clauses in the extracted scope, written before any intent law exists.
  - The comments are Solidity `///` runs and `/** */` blocks, and Go `//` runs directly above a declaration.
  - Comments are cut into segments at NatSpec tags and blank lines, and segments into sentences.
  - A sentence is a clause if it holds a marker from a fixed list.
- **Validity rules for an intent law:**
  - W1: checker evidence;
  - W2: a faithful review by a reviewer other than the compiler;
  - W3: its clauses are atomized clauses.
- **Measures.** Coverage (the paper's intent-law coverage), compiled coverage, precision and recall of the atomization against the census, the review rate, control_recall, false_obstruction and corpus recall.
- **Thresholds.** Coverage at least 0.3, precision at least 0.8, review at least 0.9, control_recall at least 0.75, and false_obstruction = 0.

### Truth

  - Rational: free_trigger, profit, recoup and deep.
  - Irrational, the negative controls: trusted_only and costly.
  - UNKNOWN: incomparable.
  - Each truth is computed twice, from the document alone: by the instrument's enumeration of every schedule, and by holon's depth-first solver. The two agree on all 14.
  - recoup tests that the payoff is read at the schedule's end. In deep, the untrusted strategy is longer than the horizon while the witness is short, which tests bounded results read as global (L17).

## The adversarial phase

I ran it myself, without a subagent: probe scripts against the instrument, against holon's stub, and against HOLON's own committed records.

**Before the freeze, folded into the prereg:**
1. **A constant engine passed the strict-accuracy bar.** An engine that answers "rational" for every control scored 0.571 strict accuracy, which met the drafted 0.5. The bar is now 0.75, and the majority-baseline rule also catches the engine (8 correct is not above 8).
2. **Payoff and horizon errors.** An engine that reads the payoff when the predicate first holds, and one that searches only up to the horizon, both met every accuracy bar. Only the zero-count rules catch them, through recoup and deep. Those families exist for that reason, and false_irrational = 0 is a threshold.
3. **Dropping a premise raised coverage.** On the fixture, L9's headline coverage rose from 0.20 to 0.25. Base laws that lose their premise now stay in the headline denominator, uncovered, and base coverage is reported apart.
4. **A fitted parameter went undetected.** A window chosen from the evaluation data but declared a premise could not be seen. R6 now requires the parameters that the measuring key pinned.
5. **One-class history.** An "always available" module validates on history that holds only one class, such as I59's blobs: 62 192 of 62 192 were retrievable. R5 now requires at least 5 observations of each observed class.
6. **Circular truth.** `holon/historical/fetch.py` chose the beacon or the archive by its own `RETENTION_SLOTS`. The snapshot's source field is therefore not truth for a retention window. R1 and `known_inadmissible` record this.
7. **Weak transcriptions.** Laws that are all PROVED reached 0.92 coverage on the fixture. Only control_recall catches them, so each metric gained an overall verdict that needs every threshold.
8. **Unconstrained value flows.** V8 now pins the value-flow document.
9. **Splitting A-env.** No path existed for I57 to split `A-env` into its L1 and call terms. Extension files, pinned by the measuring key, may add ids but never change a base entry.
10. **Corpus feasibility.** I read the 20 corpus texts as a feasibility check, not as truth. About 4–6 entries document a trigger by an untrusted actor with no value flow (peer-to-peer and view-call denial of service), and about 1 documents a costly trigger. Corpus accuracy will probably be `insufficient_n` (gap E-G3), so constructed and documented truth are never pooled.

**Each metric can pass and can fail:**
- the clean fixtures meet every threshold of L6, L9 and L10;
- each gaming fixture misses;
- the constant-irrational engine misses every L6 accuracy bar.
