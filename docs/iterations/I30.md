**The M3 model generator** (`holon/sorts/model3.py` and `prelude3.py`; `holon model --milestone M3 [--untyped]`):
- **Words** are eight U32 limbs, `W{l0..l7}`. Both kernels evaluate a concrete step in polynomial time (N8). The
  arithmetic and byte primitives check under bend2 and BendTT on concrete values.
- **Storage** is a raw slot map, with mapping slots computed through the law's keccak256 table.
- **Every record of the document is compiled**: checks, writes, events, memory regions, and loop records with their
  carried values, exits, writes and checks. Only A-env terms are left to the environment: msg, block and tx members,
  blob hashes, and external-call answers.
- **`Reach`** starts at R0, the binding's deployment state. `holon/sorts/deploy3.py` captures it from a generated
  Foundry test that inherits the binding's harness: 18 slots, 7 preimages and the immutables, written to
  `<model>.init.json`. RStep is one step of any action.
- **The typed section** holds:

  - 11 typed operations from the §7.2 table;

- **The ghost section** (below the marker) holds the generated companion `gc_batchBlobVersionedHashes`, its ghost
  step, and `coerce_<x>`/`chit_<x>` for each law.
- **`--untyped`** writes the same machine without the sort section, plus I15's `site_<x>`/`hit_<x>` for the ratio
  site.
- **Output files.** Bend import paths cannot contain a dot, so the model is written as
  `morph_rollup_extracted.bend`. `morph_rollup.extracted.bend` is a link to it, and the records name the link

**The typed model as an evaluator** (`holon/js/eval3.mjs`, `holon/sorts/evaluate3.py`;
`holon fragments --evaluator <model>.bend`): the compiled model runs in node, with hooks for the hash tables and
external calls.

**The refuter** (`holon/sorts/search3.py`): I15's S1–S3 over the compiled typed model from R0, with the sidecar's
budget.
- **S1** synthesizes one step by coordinate moves:
  - first the best of the failing check's directed values, then value-set moves, then two-coordinate repairs;
  - its inputs are generic: the state's words, preimages and stored addresses, the document's literals, ±1
    neighbours, and the clocks and durations the frozen sorts name;
  - a bytes argument also gets the preimages of the hashes the candidate's own evaluation computed.
    commitBatchWithProof must be handed back the header it commits.
  - a step that succeeds but is not yet accepted gets two-coordinate repairs for its focus leaves, the best 8. An
    example is a version change, then the recomputed header.
- **S2** is breadth first over distinct states (storage, ghost store, clock). Each parent's successors are generated
  when its turn comes.
- **S3** inserts one or two successful steps at every position of the other entry points' prefixes, the end first.
  It runs the rest of the prefix again, then the entry step.
- **Each entry point is searched on its own model**, from R0 with the initial tables.
- **Call answers come from the I20 corpus run's hints** (`results/M3/sorts/refinement/hints.json`). Before a
  counter-law is emitted, its calls are replaced by the real harness's answers. Its hash tables are the real hash of
  every key the trace looks up, its entry step's keys included.
- **`holon run --resume`** checkpoints each search under `~/.holon/cache/run3/<law>-<key>`. The key covers the law,
  its statement, the typed model, the initial state, the hints, the budget and the generator files.
  - An interrupted run resumes and ends where the uninterrupted one ends. It restores the BFS level, the states tried,
    the counters and the hash tables, and rebuilds each state by replaying its trace.
  - Scratch checks: a search on the rollup with budgets 40/25 and 120/3 was interrupted after its 2nd, 3rd and 4th
    checkpoints (S2 at depth 2, parents 0, 1 and 2, and S3). Each resume gives the same witness, stage, bound and
    final hash tables.
  - The 279 law, run uninterrupted and run killed then resumed, gives the same record, apart from its timings. This
    was checked with the code of `32a8c07`; the later fixes do not touch the checkpoint logic.
  - The records do not depend on it. The gate's G1 does not use it.

**Counter-laws, laws, records, replay** (`holon/sorts/proofs3.py`, `holon/sorts/run3.py`; `holon run`/`holon replay`
for typed milestones):
- **Counter-laws.** A counter-law proof states the witness's hash table, the R0/RStep chain and the entry step. Words
  are written `M.w_of_limbs([..])`, so that a literal list counts as one term.
- **Records.** They are v1 and v2, with the coercion, the site, I19's coverage field and the extracted ledger.

- **`--jobs N`** decides N laws at a time. Each law has its own model, refuter and harness. On the toy the records are
  identical to the sequential run's.

**The coercion_guard proof generator** (`proofs3.coercion_guard`):
- It mirrors each entry point's program statement by statement from `x0`: one lemma per check, matching its condition
  with its equation.
- At the hit, a failing realization contradicts one earlier passed check that orders the same two operands. It uses
  one lemma, by case on the comparison.
- It assumes no reachability. A PROVED law gets a checked, reachable inhabitant of `chit_<x>` from the search.

## Surprises

1. **BendTT cannot check one rollup step.**
   - bend2 accepts every rollup counter-law whose witness holds, in 40–60 s.
   - The pinned kernel stops on all of them. On bend2's `-o .bendtt` translation of the 279 counter-law, the kernel
     binary (`~/.bend/bendtt/b9f3e0253abffb11/bendtt`) says `SOME PROOFS FAIL / In ...cl_l_sort_tag_rollup_279_e01ea84a:
     out of fuel`, and bend2 reports this as its "mismatch" message.
   - The fuel is `FUEL : Nat := 400000000` in `bendtt.lean`, a constant of the formalized kernel, so it cannot be
     raised without unpinning the checker.
   - A bisection with per-statement probes (scratch only) finds the step:
     - the commitBatch program from statement 9 down passes;
     - from statement 10 (a storage write of the header encoding) it runs out;
     - the write's key and value evaluate under BendTT on their own.
   - So the cost is in how the context record grows across statements. The kernel is substitution-based, and each
     statement re-evaluates the projections of the previous one.
   - Every arithmetic, byte and memory primitive of the prelude checks under BendTT on concrete values.
   - The toy's counter-laws and its coercion_guard proof pass both kernels.
2. **The kept-register law is false in the model, not just unproved.** See Questions. The prereg expects tag_exact
   PROVED on the toy.
3. **A Bend import path cannot contain a dot.** `morph_rollup.extracted.bend` cannot be imported, so the model is
   written as `morph_rollup_extracted.bend`, and `<model>.bend` is a relative link to it.
   - Bend realpaths imports, and the tests resolve them.
   - The records name the link (`32a8c07`; the first records named the file behind it).
4. **R5 found a real model bug.** A check that failed inside a loop body ended the body's chain without the loop's
   decision. The loop then ran to its fuel: 12 boundary-corpus steps, all with numL1Messages > 0 and a reverting
   getCrossDomainMessage. Fixed in `1f05cdc`: the body ends in `lz`, and `lb` decides after it.
5. **My keccak-table hygiene broke two things, and the rollup found both.** The search dropped the keys a candidate
   learned unless the candidate was kept (`ec0b930`).
   - **S1 lost the preimages a candidate computed.** commitBatchWithProof must be handed back the header it commits
     (its `_batchHeader`), so every commitBatchWithProof synthesis stopped at :417.
     - The first 406 run found no witness in 50 minutes at depth 2.
     - The earlier :363 witness at commitBatchWithProof existed only because a commitBatch successor's header
       happened to stay in the table.
     - Fixed in `32a8c07`: S1 offers each candidate the preimages its own evaluation computed. A step's key changes
       when it is evaluated (the call answers are recorded in its env), and the first version of the fix missed that.
   - **A witness's hash tables lost its entry step's keys** (`settle` evaluated that step through the forgetting
     path).
     - Every hash the entry step computes read 0 in the counter-law, and bend2 judged the entry step on those hashes.
     - On the 406 candidate this changed the verifier's calldata. Bend then found a hint's answer instead of the
       deployment's `invalid proof`, and bend2 accepted a counter-law whose step reverts on the real contract.
     - The real-contract replay showed it: model and real both `rev invalid proof`, calls not agreeing.
     - Fixed in `615b8db`. The settle fixpoint keeps those keys and runs on copies of the steps, so a lookup made
       while the table is incomplete never adds an answer to the witness.
6. **A succeeding step had no two-coordinate repairs.** At a plateau (the step succeeds but the coercion holds) the
   synthesis stopped (`28c5c0f`), but the 406 candidate needs a version change and then the recomputed header. Repairs
   are back for focus-leaf moves only, the best 8.
7. **P2's verifier.**
   - Every verifyAggregateProof call in the I20 corpus was accepted, so the hints answer "accepted" for any proof (the
     selector rule).

### The diagnosis

The pinned BendTT kernel normalizes the argument of every reusable binder of a closed term by walking it whole.
- Walks of data that was already normal were about 98% of the kernel's work on one rollup step.
- One commitBatch step from the deployment took 1.01e9 kernel steps. The fuel is 4e8 per conversion.
- Evidence: the agent's profile, scratch `a30/transcript_used.txt`, before and after.

### The fix (commit 9862a6c; generators only, no kernel change)

- **affine3.py:** every action context binder is affine. A leaf block opens the context once and binds each field at
  its exact quantity.
- **prelude3.py:**
  - Lists are recursed on affinely: slot map, hash table, call answers, memory and bytes.
  - Bytes and limbs are built bit by bit.
  - Word addition skips the U32 adder on a zero limb, and word equality stops at the first differing limb.
  - The hash table is searched by key bytes, last byte first.
- **model3.py:** the model version is `holon-model-m3/0.2`, and the bytes of a literal word are folded to a literal.
- **proofs3.py, run3.py and search3.trace_states:** a counter-law (and an inhabitant) states the literal state after each
  prefix step. Each premise is a closed lemma of one step, and the refutation's binder only rewrites with it. So no
  kernel conversion runs the model lazily or re-runs a prefix.
