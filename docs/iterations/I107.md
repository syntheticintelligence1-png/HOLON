## Surprises

1. **Extraction reach on the sealed targets is high, and derive reach is zero.** 87 of 95 contract documents extract:
   I108's remappings and submodules, with a repository's own tree, are enough. Then the total sort pass, run over a
   whole system's documents in one milestone, does not finish in 1,800 s on either target. That is a scale miss, which
   no corpus entry exercised.
2. **No driver composes I103-I105 end to end.** `holon eval holon` records the inferred roles but never installs them.
   Route C (I104's smoke) installs them, but its derive refuses without a `link.json`. Inferred roles reach no rule
   producer on any target here.
3. **I108's base protocol carried `stop_after: derive`** (A4), and the go-machines driver takes `nil_maps` only through
   `--protocol` (A3).
4. **Route B's typed stage refuses every Go machine** once the sort stage passes, so Go-only systems still derive no law.
