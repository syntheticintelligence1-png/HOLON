## Surprises

1. **No UNDO proof generator is emitted over the typed M3 model.** This is the builder's diagnosis, recorded in each
   UNKNOWN record's `attempts`. The finisher checked that each record carries it, not that it is right.
   - **Noninterference (residue laws, and pair laws with residues).** The typed model keys every mapping slot through
     the law's keccak256 table, a forall binder that NZ alone constrains, so "another key" is not "another slot".
     Under a table that maps F's slot at k onto a slot that a dead reader reads, `set_F(s, k, v)` changes that reader's
     observation. The residue claim would then be false in the model as stated. The map lemmas set_d and nth_d need
     structural storage keys (HUMAN_ANSWERS Q21).
   - **The restore proof (challenger pairs).** When the register was unwritten in s, s2's slot map holds an explicit
     entry that s lacks (`kw_set` appends). So s2 equals s only extensionally, and the proof needs a congruence lemma
     per action, which this build does not generate.
   - **Consequences.** P5, P6, P8, P10 and R2 miss on their PROVED parts.
   - **The first point means the dead residue laws, as derived, may be unprovable in the typed model, not just
     unproved.** That is a statement-level question for the human (below), not something to fix by adding a premise.
2. **commitBatchWithProof's witnesses do not run on the deployment.** The model's answer for the verifier call accepts
   every synthesized proof. The binding's verifier stand-in accepts only a proof whose first byte is 0x01
   (`HolonVerifierOracle`, replay/M1/solidity/HolonTraceReplay.t.sol:49). This is the same obstacle as I30's P2.
   - The builder's draft also noted, from a scratch script, that the model's call data for the verifier call carry
     zero words where the real call carries the public-input hash. The finisher did not re-verify this. If true, it is
     a model-side disagreement to look at.
3. **The UNDO search is slow per candidate.** In about 46 minutes each, CSR's and addChallenger's searches covered
   50 to 57 thousand candidates and never left the first prefix state. At that rate the preregistered 200000
   candidates take about 3 hours per law. The 23:00 time box, not the budget, ended four searches. Every such record
   says that the budget was not exhausted.
4. **The basis instrument's input fingerprints went stale in the base.** `undo_basis.json` fingerprints the four M1 UNDO records (BD, BVH, CSR, cancel); for example it fingerprints
   results/M1/L_undo_BD.json, and F30, A30 and F31 rewrote it (generator_sha256 and seconds) after the I27 prereg.
   R4's first check therefore fails on the base, independently of I27.
