### NAT: naturality defect mass (stage 1)

A NAT square's observation is its claim's (I29). It has these components:
- the end version of each side;
- the success of each step that the two sides share;
- each storage word that either end state holds (an absent word counts as zero).

The mass is the number of components on which the two paths end differently at the witness. It is read from the model's two runs that replay the witness in `replay/M3/nat/report.json` (bound by its witness sha256 and trace, as `holon.rules.nat_replay` binds them). This follows BUILD_PLAN I44's reading of the mass "on its witness state".
