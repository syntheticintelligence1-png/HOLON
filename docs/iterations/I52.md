# I52 (M4): baselines on Morph and the corpus

Nine baselines were run at equal budget on Morph (one target, the pinned checkout 3e44e79) and on I48's corpus (20 pre-fix
commits): slither, echidna, medusa, hevm, halmos, go_fuzz, two_cycle, spec_mining and agent_review. All findings use
one schema, and a blinded adjudicator decides each match. The held-out run is not part of I52: it moved to I52b
(below, Not done).

- **Prereg:** commit 8347767b, key `I52` of `spec/M4/predictions.json`. It holds the protocol
  (`spec/M4/lib/I52_baselines.json`), schema, anchors, prompts, and the instrument `tests_m4/_i52.py`.
- **Contract:** `tests_m4/test_baselines_i52.py` (rules B1–B9, predictions P1–P8). `python -m holon audit prereg
  --milestone M4` prints PREREG OK.
- **Results:** `results/eval/morph/<baseline>.json` and `results/eval/corpus/<baseline>.json`, each with a
  `.timing.json` beside it, plus `results/eval/holon_budget.json`.

## What ran, and at what budget

- **Equal budget.** Every tool baseline got wall clock on one pinned core (taskset, GOMAXPROCS=1). Setup was outside the
  budget, capped at 3600 s. A target's units shared its budget sequentially.
- **Corpus budget:** 300 s per entry.
- **Morph budget:** 10620 s per baseline, by the preregistered rule `60 * ceil(H / 60)`.
  - H = 10570.197 s: the recorded `from_scratch_gate_s` of M0 (101.641), M1 (119.873) and M2 (224.088), plus M3
    measured here.
  - The M3 term was a full `holon gate --milestone M3` on a clean worktree of 4fd64a52 with nothing else running:
    10124.595 s wall, 11783.681 s CPU.
  - That run exited 1 with G1 alone failing (Surprises).
- **Morph budget use:**
  - echidna and medusa used it all (six units of about 1770 s each).
  - hevm timed out on four of its six units.
  - go_fuzz used 9106 s.
  - halmos, slither, two_cycle and spec_mining finished early.
- **Non-vacuity.** Every baseline ran on at least half of its applicable targets on both systems. On the corpus,
  go_fuzz errored on 3 of its 14 applicable entries (Surprises); every other tool baseline ran on all of its own.
- **Reviewers (agent_review): two, one fresh reviewer per system.** Tools were Read, Grep and Glob only. The model was
  observed at run time and kept outside the repository. Each reviewer's budget was HOLON's wall clock for that system,
  and neither reviewer used it:
  - **Corpus:** I48's isomorphic renamings only (seed `I52:<entry>`, 31 files). It finished on its own in 445 s of its
    6000 s budget (20 × 300 s). It took 78 turns and about 29k output tokens, with 9.1M cache-read input tokens.
  - **Morph:** the scope files at 3e44e79. It finished on its own in 167 s of its 10620 s budget, with about 13k output
    tokens.
- **HOLON's corpus budget (I51) is not compared.** `results/eval/holon/` records no wall clock per entry, so the
  per-entry comparison stays pending.

## Surprises

1. **slither detects a corpus entry (P3 missed).**
   - slither's stock `locked-ether` detector reports, on c05, that `SuperPermissionedDisputeGame` has a payable
     `initialize` and no function to withdraw ether. That is the entry's defect: a nonzero initialization bond trapped
     in each clone.
   - The finding is located on the fix and confirmed by the blinded adjudicator. A stock single-component detector found
     a single-component defect.
2. **two_cycle's round-trip failures are not O2 (P2 missed).**
   - The 2-cycle harness found value-cycle failures in `BatchHeaderV0.Bytes` and `BatchHeaderV1.Bytes` against the
     `BatchHeaderBytes` getters. Both are located on O2's anchor (and on O37's).
   - The adjudicator judged that neither describes O2. The located-but-unconfirmed pair is recorded in the results.
3. **spec_mining misses c06 (P5 missed).**
   - c06 is an unchecked type assertion. B1 includes comma-ok type assertions, but none of the miner's 11 c06
     deviants (support ≥ 3, confidence ≥ 0.75) lies within the fix's window.
4. **The stock harness rule leaves the symbolic executor mostly vacuous on Morph.**
   - The frozen harness deploys each contract without a proxy and never calls `initialize`, so owners and pause state
     are unset.
   - halmos: 53 of 66 checks reverted on every path. By contract: Rollup 22 of 24, Submitter 12 of 13,
     L1CrossDomainMessenger 8 of 11, L1MessageQueueWithGasPriceOracle 7 of 9, L1Sequencer 4 of 5,
     MultipleVersionRollupVerifier 0 of 4.
   - The fuzzers' summaries show only passing assertion tests, which fits mostly reverting calls.
   - The rule stands as preregistered (the lead's ruling). An initializing rerun would be a new key, not a change to
     I52. I52b found the same on ScrollChain. This is a threat to the Solidity tools' negative results, listed under
     Threats.
5. **HOLON's M3 gate does not pass at the base.**
   - Measured twice, the full M3 gate fails G1 with the same eight differences: the extracted model init, three sort
     records, two undo records, term_size, a FACT `FileExistsError` and the bundle laws' generator stamps.
   - G1 is the check CI leaves out of M3 (I97). Every other check passed.
   - The first measurement, on a copy of my working tree, ran 7882.771 s while my builds ran beside it. It was not used.
   - By the lead's ruling, the budget test accepts exit 1 only with G1 alone failing, every check listed, and G1's
     detail recorded verbatim (`holon_budget.json`; amendment in `spec/M4/amendments/I52.jsonl`). The rule and the
     value are unchanged.
   - H includes a G1 that ended in a regeneration error, so its share may undercount what a passing G1 would take.
     The gate report gives no per-check time, so G1's own time cannot be stated.
6. **Runner defects found during the runs, each fixed in the generic runner.** In each case the affected raw records
   were discarded before any result was assembled, and the runs were repeated.
   - A checkout race: a waiting thread deleted a checkout in use.
   - One import file for all scope files: two Compound files both declare `PotLike`.
   - The same file compiled under two source names (`holon_target/src/..` and `src/..`).
   - Missing imports for inherited functions' struct types; user-defined value types; `address payable`; pre-0.8
     syntax.
   - echidna and medusa sharing one harness directory and removing each other's `crytic-export`.
   - echidna's default 50000-call test limit ending units long before their share. The budget is now its only stop, as
     medusa's testLimit 0 already was.
   - Fuzzer start-up time outside `--timeout`, which pushed c02's 39 units past 300 s. The start-up is now subtracted,
     and units past the budget are recorded as skipped: c02 echidna used 298 s, with its last 7 units skipped.
   - A full disk, which failed c18's Go builds and c20's submodules. My own finished checkouts were removed and both
     re-run.
   - A container restart, which killed the first Morph run 2.6 h in. slither, hevm and halmos were kept; the rest
     re-ran.
7. **`assemble --check` was inverted.** It treated "differs" as ok. Fixed by the same one-line change as I52b's
   f84226467f63, checked both ways: identical files give OK, a tampered message gives INCOMPLETE. Both systems pass the
   fixed check.
8. **The corpus reviewer seems to know the upstream projects.** Its answer speaks of "upstream", an "injected change"
   and checks that "no longer" exist. The renaming hides identifiers, not the code's shape (Threats).
9. **echidna 2.4.0 refuses `--seed` together with `--timeout`.** The protocol's `--seed 1` was dropped, and the record
   says so (`seeds`).
10. **The adjudicator ran as one process per located group, with the frozen single-group template.** The protocol's
    text speaks of batches of up to 25 groups. 12 + 47 groups were adjudicated (corpus, Morph), each on one attempt.
11. **The commit trailer.** The first local attempt at the prereg commit named a model in its trailer. It was never
    pushed; it was reset and recommitted with the plain `Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>` trailer.
12. **B7's two amendments.**
    - B7 first diffed 4fd64a52..HEAD, so merging the lead counted the lead's 61 audit/ changes as I52's. An edit of
      the test was refused by the prereg audit, which tracks test sources.
    - Under the lead's ruling, B7 was amended to I52's own non-merge first-parent commits.
    - It was then amended again, to the range ending at this report's commit (R52), so later landings are not counted.
    - The protected prefixes and the claim are unchanged.

## Threats to validity

- **Vacuous harness.** The Solidity tools' "nothing found" on Morph and the corpus is weak evidence, because most
  harness paths revert before the code under test (Surprise 4).
- **Possible contamination.** The reviewer may recall the public fixes (Surprise 8). The corpus result for
  agent_review is an upper bound for a reviewer who does not.
- **One reviewer per system.** Each finished in well under its budget. A second reviewer, or one pushed to use its
  budget, may differ.
- **The adjudicator is one model, the same tag A as the reviewer.** It is blinded to the baseline's name but not to
  the model.
- **Unequal CPU.** HOLON's M3 run used 11784 s of CPU over 10125 s of wall clock. Each baseline got one core for the
  same wall clock.
- **The anchors decide "located" on Morph.** O10 has no anchor, so it cannot be detected by any baseline.
