   - **Equivariance (`equivariance`, 5 laws, §§6.1–6.2)**:
     - `core_equivar_compose`: horizontal composition of equivariant steps (`f(s1(s2(a))) == t1(t2(f(a)))`).
     - `core_equivar_whisker`: vertical whiskering of equivariant morphisms (`g(f(s(a))) == u(g(f(a)))`).
     - `core_equivar_obs`: observation invariance under equivariant step + naturality (`obs_B(f(s(a))) == obs_A(a)`).
     - `core_equivar_pred`: Boolean invariant transport along an equivariant morphism (`Q(f(s(a))) == True{}`).
     - `core_equivar_homotopy`: 2-cell homotopy transport of equivariance across a pointwise equal source step (`s1(a) == s2(a)` and `f(s2(a)) == t(f(a))` imply `f(s1(a)) == t(f(a))`).
   - **2-categorical Pasting (`pasting`, 5 laws, §6.4)**:
     - `core_paste_grid_2x2`: 2x2 grid pasting of two horizontal by two vertical 2-cells (`g1(f1(a)) == g3(f3(a))`).
     - `core_paste_h_3`: 3-step horizontal pasting with a whiskered right 2-cell (`h1(g1(f1(a))) == h2(g2(f2(a)))`).
     - `core_paste_v_obs`: vertical pasting of two observation-commuting triangles (`obs_C(g(f(a))) == obs_A(a)`).
     - `core_paste_square_trans`: vertical composition of two natural-transformation squares (`w(g(f(a))) == g1(f1(u(a)))`).
     - `core_paste_whisker_interchange`: interchange of left-whiskered 2-cell and right-whiskered 2-cell (`g1(f1(a)) == g2(f2(a))`).
   - Emitted into `gen/M3/core/{LAWS,PROOF}.bend`, bringing `gen/M3/core` to `31 + 12 + 10 = 53` laws while keeping `gen/M2/core/{LAWS,PROOF}.bend` untouched at 31 laws.
