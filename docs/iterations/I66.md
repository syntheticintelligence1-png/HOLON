## What I66 shows, and what it does not

- **It shows** that a window view can be modelled like a fact document, with no fact document changed. M2's evidence calculus applies to the view as written: the guard-relative kinds (E3, E4, E6) and E2's carrier relations come out as the abstraction's, less what the removed requires carried. A law over the view, the first being I65's, now gets computed evidence instead of none.
- **It does not show** that the view is faithful to the contract. The view is the abstraction without its unmatched requires, as holon differential made it. I66 models what the view says.
- **It adds no E5 for window views.** E5 reads the frozen sorts assignment's model of a document's stem, and I13's pass assigns sorts to fact documents only. So over the view, a law whose only atoms stronger than E6 would be E5 atoms has max E6: a REFUTED one is triaged `intent_question` where over the abstraction it would be a finding.
- **The M1 laws' records over the view stay uncovered**, as R1 claims. They are in `results/M2/extracted_m1` and name the abstraction's path with the view's sha256, so L1 finds no model for them. GEVID reads none of them, so nothing fails.
