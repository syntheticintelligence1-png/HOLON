## Done

- **`holon/gen/eqlib.py`, the Base lemma library.** It covers U32 as Base's Word of 32 Bools:
  - `is_eq` against `xor` and `or`;
  - addition with carry and `inc`;
  - subtraction with borrow;
  - a byte assembled by shifts and masks, against bit by bit.

  Each lemma is stated for every input of its types. It is proved by induction over `Word(n)`, whose step is a
  finite lemma checked by exhaustive case analysis, or by honest finite case analysis. The text is fixed: every name
  starts with `lb`, and no model, target or definition is named (tested). It checks alone in bend2 and BendTT.
- **`holon/gen/eqprove.py` and `holon/gen/eqterms.py`, the proof classes.** They hold:
  - case split with reflection (`U32.is_eq(a, b) == True` → `a == b`, then the same for `w_eq`);
  - lemma discovery, with accumulator specialization (one lemma per constructor of an extra constructor-valued
    argument, such as `kw_put`'s `done`, by mutual induction);
  - bit-by-bit evaluation, and use of the Base rules.

  They are wired into `holon.gen.equivalence` as further classes. The F31 criterion is unchanged: every root is
  extensionally equal on every input, and family roots are inter-derivable both ways.
- **`holon/sorts/prelude3_lemmas.py`, the list lemmas `h_get`'s rewrite needs.** They are stated for all lists:
  - `lp_rev_rev` and `lp_rev2` (reversal);
  - `lp_beq_refl`, `lp_beq_sound` and `lp_beq_rev` (`b_eq`);
  - `lp_filt_nil` and `lp_filt_con` (a filter over cons).

  They are used only for a model whose prelude definitions are the prelude's own text (`applies`). They check in
  both kernels.
- **`tests_m3/test_eqprove.py`** (7 tests). It covers the library in both kernels, a false lemma refused, the prelude
  lemmas guarded, the A30 helpers proved, planted non-equivalences not proved, determinism, and no target names.
- **Finisher commits:**
  - `3d7288a`: progress lines on stderr behind `HOLON_EQV_PROGRESS`. They were uncommitted in the WIP tree; the
    change is sound and nothing else reads them.
  - `d1ad03b`: BendTT seconds recorded per root in the equivalence result.
- **M1 and M2 records regenerated** (`holon run --milestone M1`, `M2`). `holon/gen/equivalence.py` and the modules
  it imports are in the generator closure (`holon.records.generator_files`), so every record's `generator_sha256`
  changed. This is the same step F31 took. Without it, M1's G1 fails on its records; that was observed before the
  regeneration.

## Surprises

- The real pair module is about 25 times the prelude pair. Proving the prelude pair takes 88 s; the cone of the
  first law did not finish in 84 min. The cost is in the round structure (a bend2 re-check of the whole LEMMAS.bend
  per round), not in any one lemma.
- `holon/gen/equivalence.py` and its new imports are in the M1/M2 generator closure. Any change to the equivalence
  generator invalidates every M1 and M2 record's `generator_sha256`, so the records must be regenerated.
- The first frozen-suite run under heavy machine load reported one failure. A verbose rerun passed. A timing-sensitive
  test is the likely cause, but the failing name was not captured.
