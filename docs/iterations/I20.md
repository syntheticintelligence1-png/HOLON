## Surprises

1. **The one non-ghost status difference is an artifact of span matching, not a difference in behaviour.** L_disch_delay_nofree is PROVED on M1 and REFUTED on the view. Its witness is `sendMessage; commitBatch(n=1); wait(21); commitBatch(n=1)`. The second commit pops a message the queue does not hold. M1 forbids it with the require at `L1MessageQueueWithGasPriceOracle.sol:108`, which sits in the queue contract. The Rollup document enforces the same thing as the success of its external call at the call site (a `callok` check), so no extracted check of commitBatch has the span `:108`. The contract's rule ("guard_missing: an M1 require with no extracted guard of the same action, matched by span") therefore removes the require from the view. P5 passes as written, but the REFUTED says nothing about the real Rollup: the raw model reverts there whenever the call fails. The other guard_missing deltas have three sources:
   - an oracle require with no source span (`oracle: IRollupVerifier.verifyAggregateProof`);
   - M1's convention of locating a modifier's require at the function that uses it (setPause's `PausableUpgradeable.sol:94`/`:106`). The extracted check is at `:77`/`:84`, with `:94`/`:106` in its call stack;
   - M1's commitState guard 11 (the `l1msg delay` require), whose `src` names commitBatch's lines `Rollup.sol:262-266`. commitState's own check is at `:281-283`, and the extracted document has it there. This looks like a source-annotation slip in the frozen M1 facts (Question 1).

   I kept the contract's literal rule and did not change the delta derivation after seeing this outcome.
2. **The window check had been vacuous.** The paused integrator's `window_check.json` compared 0 states: A's handoff asked B to write the corpus, B did not, and the integrator had not either. Now it compares 548 states with 0 disagreements. That required learning the corpus's token encodings from the steps' inputs. Version-2 blob hashes are decoded through the abstraction's own stand-in (`blob_hash` at version 2 against keccak256 of the blob hashes). Without that encoding, 178 fields stayed undecoded.
3. **Guard coverage rose from 157 to 209 with two search fixes.**
   - The targeting search had no mutation of `msg.data.length`, so all 29 calldatasize checks stayed unfalsified.
   - Synthesized bases, for actions the corpus never calls, took another call's environment.
