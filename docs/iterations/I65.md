## How trace_bound proves the law

The derived law `L_contract_C_o12` (laws/M2/L_contract_C_o12.json, IR 0.3) has the binders `forall p: Trace`, `forall k: Nat`. Its three conjuncts hold on the state `run(p, s0)`, where `g` is the ghost sum of `W` over the successful `challengeState` steps of `p` from `s0`:
- (a) `win_at(win, k).bdf <= win_at(win, k).bdo + F + g`: each window entry's finalize deadline is at most its commit-time deadline plus `F` plus the extensions so far;
- (b) `inch -> W <= g`: during a challenge, at least one `W` has been added;
- (c) `inch -> nth_ch(chs, bc).s <= t`: the open challenge started at or before now.

`trace_bound` (`holon/gen/trace_bound.py`) proves the conjunction as an inductive invariant of every run. It searches for no strengthening, so the claim must be inductive as stated; the contract states (b) and (c) for that reason. The proof is built in six parts:
- **The invariant.** `tb_I(st, g)` is the three conjuncts, written with the statement's own `ex_` helpers over a state and a running sum. (a)'s `forall k` sits inside it.
- **The start state.** `s0` is handled by computation, with (a) split as far as the initial window list.
- **One lemma per action** (10 actions):
  - For 8 actions the transport needs no value the decision carries, so the lemma is generalised over the decision (`tb_x_<a>`).
  - `commitBatch` and `commitState` carry a payload (their guards' lets). Their lemmas walk the decide chain one guard at a time (14 and 12 nodes, `tb_w_<a>_<i>`), with the lets inlined.
  - Each node keeps its guard's equation. This is Bend's convoy pattern: node i takes `{C_i == c}`, matches `c`, and passes on the equations of the guards before it. On the Ok branch, the guards that compare Nats are facts.
- **Carrying each conjunct** through the action's effects uses:
  - Bool case splits (19 helpers `tb_c<N>`);
  - the core library's Nat lemmas and the file's own;
  - list predicates (snoc, take, drop, the library's `set_default`);
  - the pointwise sidecar `spec/M1/lib/morph_rollup.pointwise.json` for `bump_f` and `clear_chs`. Its statements are proved in the same file from the builtins' own definitions, never assumed: `bump_f` raises `bdf` by at most its increment and keeps every other field, and `clear_chs` keeps or defaults each entry.
- **The ghost.** On a successful `challengeState` the running sum grows by `W`, which pays for `bump_f`'s increment in (a) and establishes (b). A reverting step keeps the state and adds 0.
- **Induction on the trace**, generalised over the start state and the running sum, with the `Nat.add` identities proved in the file.

The file has 902 lines. Bend checks it in 0.6 s and BendTT (`--verdict`) in 1.2 s, and it imports exactly its `LAWS.bend`. The generator reads only the law IR, the compiled model's decide chains, the target library and the pointwise sidecar. It names no law, contract or target, proves the second claim `C_o12_open`, and returns no proof for `C_o12_tight`, whose bound is false.

## What I65 shows, and what it does not

- **It shows** that a cumulative bound over unboundedly many steps can be proved by a generated, law-agnostic proof class and checked by Bend's kernel:
  - the bound is O12's: deadlines extended by W per successful challenge, over every trace of the window view;
  - the invariant is the claim itself (no strengthening search), with a ghost counter as the measure;
  - the proof is not hand-written, and the class proves a second claim it was not shaped for (C_o12_open).
- **It shows** that a contract can turn a real REFUTED finding (`L_undo_cancel` on the view, concretized on the real Rollup by I40) into `intended_bounded`. The bound is proved, and the gate checks the link (G10) at every run.
- **It does not show** O12 on the extracted Rollup itself (M3). The Rollup's words, free per-step timestamps, owner-changeable F and W, and hashed mapping slots make the M3 statement different. The design for that (`I33`) found the three conjuncts false there as stated, and depends on the Q21 storage fix (I68).
- **It does not show** that the class is general. It carries `<=` comparisons and the list lemmas C_o12 needs (snoc, take, drop, set_default, pointwise builtins). O2's description also names `<`, `==`, `tail` and `set_default` under the quantified binder, which the class does not carry: such a law gets no proof from it (see Not done).

## Surprises

- **P11 (the R2/R3 misses).** Adding a law to M<j> changes the derivation of every later target milestone. Every later key that adds a law to M0-M2 must predict, or regenerate, M3's derivation.
- **M3 records trail I65's closures.** I65's code is in every M3 generator closure (GEN3, GEN_FACT, GEN_UNDO, GEN_DISCH, holon-ir/0.3), and I65 re-makes no M3 record (R3). So all M3 records' `generator_sha256` trail until the next M3 re-make (I28's build). The full M3 gate's G1, which CI does not run, fails on that staleness until then. CI's M3 jobs do not see it. The same holds after I66, which says so in its prereg.
- **`holon run --milestone M2 --laws X` overwrites outputs.** It rewrites `results/M2/summary.json`, `deps.json` and `gen/M2/model/` with that law alone. I ran it once by mistake, then regenerated with full runs only.
- **`holon run` drops the `concretization` block of the REFUTED M1/M2 records it rewrites.** The final filter (`holon refute --milestone M3 --only filter`) restores it. A run without the filter would break R2's field check.
- **`results/M2/historical/laws.json` was assembled, not regenerated.** The I59 snapshot (`~/.holon/historical`) is not in this container. - **Two record fields of the derived law describe the abstraction.**
