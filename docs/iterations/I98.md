## The lead's calls on the outcome

### 1. The ghost tag: the obstruction must be observed

**The ruling.** G6 binds a REFUTED record only when its replay observes the obstruction on the real code.
- **The one exception is GHOST_ONLY.** It is family-level and mechanical: a record whose obstruction is stated over
  ghost state alone binds on calls agreeing, that is, on a passed replay.
- **It is decided from the law's IR** by `holon.replay_bind.ghost_only(law, entry)`. The violated predicate is the law's
  claim, with the coercion the claim applies expanded to its `laws/M3/DERIVATION.json` terms (its member function and
  its read).
- **The predicate is ghost-only when it names only generated ghost fields,** that is, when none of those terms reads the
  code's state or inputs (storage, map, index, param, caller, env, call, outflow). A claim that applies any other
  definition compares the code's state by its family's definition, so it is never ghost-only.
- **G6 names the rule and counts it.** On M3 the count is **0**.

**What each clause's replay observes** (`holon/replay_bind.py`; a record that several clauses judge must be observed
by each):

| clause | its replay observes the obstruction when |
|---|---|
| typed (I30) | the entry's `obstruction_observed` holds (`holon.sorts.run3.replay_entry`). A realize entry observes the real panic in its span; an as_member or a reinterpret entry observes nothing. |
| UNDO | the real runs with and without x;y diverge at `t[diverges_at]`, where the model's do |
| DISCH | `blocked_before` and `discharged_after` hold, on the real storage |
| FACT, bundle (I86), bundle2 | the replay passes: its pass rule reads the obstruction on the real code (`holon.fact.run`, `holon.bundle.replay_laws`, `holon.bundle2.laws_replay`) |
| contract | the record's replay passed (no M3 contract law is REFUTED) |
