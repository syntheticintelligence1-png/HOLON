## One rule for the final filter and G6

`holon/replay_bind.py` holds the rule.
- **Every G6 clause reports through it.** Each clause that judges a REFUTED record calls `judged(law)`, and each refusal
  is `refuse(law, why)`. The clauses are:
  - the typed I30 entries, UNDO, NAT, PAR-init and the new other-laws clause;
  - and the clauses of the gates M3's gate joins: DISCH, FACT, the contract, bundle, bundle2 and derivation laws.
- **`binding(milestone, root)`** runs G6 (`Gate3(...).run(["G6"])`) inside a collection. It returns the REFUTED records
  refused (`unbound`) and those judged (`examined`). It raises when G6 did not judge.
- **The final filter** (`holon refute --only filter`) takes every unbound M3 record out of REFUTED. The record becomes
  UNKNOWN, with a bound naming G6's refusal and a `replay_binding` attempt that keeps the witness and the replay. Its law
  is kept. The filter document lists the record under `unbound`.
- So a record the filter leaves REFUTED is one G6 binds, and G6 fails exactly when the rule would move a record (R6,
  B5).
