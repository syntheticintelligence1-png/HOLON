# I33: the O12 intent contract on M3

The prereg is the key `iterations.I33` in `spec/M3/predictions.json`. The contract is the docstring of
`tests_m3/test_o12_m3.py`, rules B1-B12. The instrument is `tests_m3/_i33.py`, with the fixture
`tests_m3/fixtures/o12_m3/`.

## Before building

- Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/M0_SPEC.md`, `docs/BEND_PRIMER.md` and the contract.
- Read the M2 contract this lifts:
  - `tests_m3/test_contracts.py` (I64: K1-K10);
  - `tests_m3/test_o12.py` (I65: O1-O9);
  - `holon/contracts.py`, `holon/gen/trace_bound.py`.
- Read I68's structural store (`tests_m3/test_structural_keys.py`, rules S1-S12). Its SK frame library, convoy, loop
  frames and bit-field lemmas are what the class builds on.
- Read I71's scope law, `L_undo_cancel_Rollup_challengeState_setPause`, and its record.
- The build starts once I68 and I71 are on the lead: merge the lead in first. The lead's lines go first in
  append-only files.

## What to build

1. **The six typed terms (B1).**
   - The schema, with docs/adoption/Q4/ kept byte-equal.
   - `holon.ir.render_term`.
   - holon.gen.refs (`storage:<F>`, `action:<A>`).
2. **Emission over the structural typed model (B2).** Statement helpers in LAWS.bend.
3. **The contract and its derivation into laws/M3 (B3, B4).** `holon derive --milestone M3` lists the contract law in
   the registry. The catalog does not change.
4. **The typed runner (B5).**
   - Dispatch intended_weak laws over holon-facts/0.3 documents from `holon run --milestone M3`.
   - The typed refuter's search.
   - The typed replay of a REFUTED witness.
5. **The class residue_bound (B6).**
   - Word↔Nat lemmas for checked arithmetic.
   - The loop lemma by fuel induction for a loop that stores at one key indexed by its induction variable.
   - SK frames, convoy, bit-field lemmas.
   - Every lemma proved in the PROOF.bend; no axiom, no escape hatch.
6. **The integration (B7-B10).** Non-vacuity, marks and G10 on M3, GEVID, records, bindings, Lean `not_run`.
7. **Outcomes.**
   - `holon contracts derive --milestone M3`, then `holon derive --milestone M3`.
   - `holon run --milestone M3 --laws L_contract_C_o12_m3`; the last step marks I71's record.
   - `holon bind --milestone M3`; `holon refute --milestone M3 --only filter`; `holon rank` and `holon report` for M3.
   - The M1, M2 and T32 re-stamps, if a build commit touches a generator closure.

## Rules

- **Never weaken the contract to make it pass.**
  - L_weak's text is fixed.
  - A conjunct the class cannot prove leaves the law UNKNOWN, and that is a miss to report.
- **No law id or contract id of I33 in holon/** (AGENTS rule 2).
- **Run your own adversarial self-check.** Do not spawn review, verification or fix agents.
- **Push only to `claude/ci-i33*`.** Never touch origin/main. Never run `tools/freeze.py write`.
- **The report** is `docs/iterations/I33.md`, ending with Done / Surprises / Questions for the human / Not done, and the
  suite's summary line.
