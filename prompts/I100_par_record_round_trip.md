# I100: rule PAR_record_round_trip

The coordinator's brief for I100 (2026-11-11), on the lead's `4fd64a52` (#131, which filed RG-M3-06 as the
candidate's second distinct finding). The lead approved the rule and set its scope.

## The candidate

`audit/rule_gaps.jsonl` holds the L7 candidate `PAR_record_round_trip`, instances RG-M3-01 to RG-M3-03 (F-M3-08: the
back-test's gap:M0:L_rt_go_v0, _v1 and _v2) and RG-M3-06 (F-M3-10: round 3's m08 and m06). Its basis: an encoder into
a byte record and a decoder given as a family of getters valid at version N satisfy dec(enc(h)) = some(view_N(h)) iff
every getter returns, on enc(h), the value view_N gives its slot. A slot is filled from a record field (PAR's round
trip states it) or with a constant (a constant read-back law states it). The record law is the conjunction over the
family at N, under the version pin, and adds no premise. Obligations (1)-(5): the family's round trips PROVED; a
constant read-back law per constant slot, PROVED; coverage against the extracted getter family, not against the laws
that exist; the record law, generated by the producer on a new catalog version and checked by Bend; the mu amendment
(dec_go to the getter family, view to the getter tuple, each constant slot to its constant) with the B3 bridges to
M0's L_rt_go_vN decided on the compiled models.
