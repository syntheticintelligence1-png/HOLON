# I7: law IR 0.3

Rendered from `docs/BUILD_PLAN.md` §4, I7. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/M0_SPEC.md` and `docs/BEND_PRIMER.md` first.

## What to build

- **`spec/schemas/law_ir_0_3.schema.json`.**
  - Types: `Nat`, `Bool`, `Bytes(n)`, `Fn<A,B>`, record refs, `State`, `Trace`, `Act`, `Reach`.
  - Premises: `eq`, `{cf: {fn, set: [terms]}}` (a finite set only) and `{hyp: {name}}`.
  - `statement.scope` is one of `{kind: unbounded}`, `{kind: bounded, H}` and `{kind: at_state, ref}`.
  - `rule_catalog_version`; structured `maps_to`.
- **`holon/ir.py`.**
  - The 0.3 statement hash is sha256 over `{ir_version, law_id, rule, rule_catalog_version, statement, refs}`. `refs` maps each named definition to the content hash of its source. The 0.1 and 0.2 hashes are unchanged.
  - `check_premises` rejects a global-injectivity premise (`set: "*"`). G3 uses it.
- **`holon/gen/mlaws.py` and the pipeline.**
  - `Fn<A,B>` binders and `cf` premises, emitted as pairwise inequality premises over the finite set.
  - **∧ counter-laws:** a REFUTED conjunction records `failing_conjunct`.
  - **∃-laws are never REFUTED.** Without a witness, an ∃-law is UNKNOWN with `bounded_negative: {H, space}`. Its negation ∀¬P is generated and decided, and recorded as `negation: {law_id, statement_hash, status}`.
  - A new proof class `act_split` proves ∀a: Act laws by matching the action constructor, then computing.
- **Non-vacuity.** A `cf` premise is inhabited by the identity function, with CF(id, S) discharged by evaluation.
- **Probe `bend/notes/p_ir03_fn_verdict/`.** A Fn binder with pairwise CF premises, checked under bend and `--verdict`.
