`spec/M4/lib/heldout_catalog_pin.json`; `holon/rules/` is never edited). The route, the sidecars and the law checks

- Sidecars Q1-Q4 are generated by rule. Every field is copied from the development milestone's sidecars where it is

  reason. None is labelled `passed`.
- Metrics: the four-diagram report gets the held-out row's obligation counts. No linearity claim is made.
